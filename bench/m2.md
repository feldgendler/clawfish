## Notes

- Plan had anticipated `-maxmoves 300` and `-resign` adjudication firing on most random-mover games — empirically neither fired across these 4 games. Random vs random naturally reaches insufficient material; sf18 mates the random mover quickly. The adjudication knobs remain in `match.sh` as a no-cost safety net for future runs.
- `ulimit -n` default on macOS (256) is too low for `fastchess`'s default concurrency; `ulimit -n 4096` is sufficient for the M2 smoke. Documented in `docs/workflow.md` runbook in a future revision if it bites again.
- `-rounds 1 -repeat` (= 2 games) chosen over `-rounds 2 -repeat` (= 4 games) because deterministic seeds + no opening book produce duplicate trajectories — see plan-review SF4 and ADR-0012 §"Smoke contract for M2".
- M2 exit criterion ("plays a complete game through `fastchess` against itself or another engine without protocol errors or illegal moves" — `docs/roadmap.md` §M2): **met.** All 4 games played through fastchess with zero protocol errors and zero illegal moves.
