## Comparison to predictions

- **CPW prior**: +30–70 Elo NMP gain at moderate engine strength (research §14).
- **Plan §11 / M5.A research §14**: "+40–70 Elo at slow TC; +20–40 Elo at fast TC" given clawfish's strong M4 ordering.
- **Actual**: +400 Elo (pentanomial CI [+285, +678]).

The actual gain dramatically exceeds the prediction. Possible explanations:
1. **Compounding interaction with M4 ordering.** With strong TT-move-first + killers + history + aspiration already in place, NMP not only cuts off early but also frees node budget for deeper iterations within the same wallclock — a multiplicative rather than additive effect.
2. **Baseline weakness amplification at the engine's current strength tier.** M4.D's chained Elo anchor sits in the 2300–2500 range (per the README's per-TC table); literature priors are typically calibrated at 2600+ where ordering quality is even tighter. NMP is the largest single search-pruning add at this strength; +400 Elo here would correspond to a much smaller marginal gain at GM-level strength.
3. **Bench result correlation.** Bench dropped 66% (15.86M → 5.35M nodes); literature predicts 30–50% reduction. Both bench and SPRT signals exceed literature priors by ~1.5×, which suggests the conditioning factors (M4 ordering + engine strength tier) consistently amplify NMP's contribution beyond reference baselines.

The Elo + bench coupling argues against measurement error — both metrics independently exceed predictions in roughly the same proportion.

## Methodology notes

- **Mixed-TC SPRT terminated before reaching the budgeted ~600 games**. With this signal magnitude, the SPRT bounds (elo0=0, elo1=10) were reached almost immediately. A tighter bound (e.g., elo0=10 elo1=20 or elo0=20 elo1=30) would give a more precise upper-end measurement but doesn't change the accept/reject verdict.
- **Per-TC sample sizes (n=2 to n=10)** are too small for narrow per-TC CIs but adequate for the doesn't-regress-anywhere floor at this signal magnitude. A follow-up fixed-game match (e.g., 200 games per TC) is optional documentation; not a gate.
- **Run took ~2 minutes wallclock** vs the ~10h prediction. The prediction assumed convergence near the boundary (~400–600 games); the actual signal magnitude let convergence happen at 22 games.
- **Adjudication parameters match the M3+ project standard** (`scripts/sprt.sh sprt` defaults).
