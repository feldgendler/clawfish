## Per-TC sample distribution — caveat

The σ-stop fired at game 56 (pair 28), before the pre-materialised SplitMix64 TC-sample sequence had given balanced bucket coverage:

| TC | W | L | D | Total games | Pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 7 | 17 | 4 | **28** | 14 |
| 20+0.2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **0** | 0 |
| 40+0.4 | 7 | 5 | 2 | **14** | 7 |
| 60+0.6 | 5 | 6 | 3 | **14** | 7 |

Expected under uniform 4-bucket sampling at 28 pairs: 7 pairs per bucket. Observed: (14, 0, 7, 7) — χ² = 14 / 3 dof, p ≈ 0.003. An unusual early-sample chi-squared outlier, but within the variance the harness's pre-materialisation rule allows for small-N draws. The M4.D mixed-TC SPRT documented the sampler as unbiased at 200 pairs (χ² = 2.92, p ≈ 0.40); this run's σ-stop fired before that asymptote was reached.

**Practical interpretation**: the converged 2601.45 figure is closer to a fast-TC rating (28 games at 10+0.1 + 14 at 40+0.4 + 14 at 60+0.6 = effective average TC ≈ 25+0.25) than a balanced mixed-TC rating. A run extended to 200+ games would re-balance, but that's not the user's request.

## Methodology notes

**1. Mixed-TC vs per-TC for rating estimation.** Per `bench/sprt/2026-05-01-m4.d-per-tc-rating-estimate.md` Observation §5: "Per-TC numbers are not directly comparable across TCs because the opponent's effective strength under UCI_LimitStrength shifts with TC. A single mixed-TC number obscures this and is not reported here." This run was requested explicitly with mixed-TC sampling, so the project's prior "don't report a single mixed number" guidance is relaxed for the M5.A milestone. The per-TC follow-up table above provides the granular interpretation.

**2. The fast-TC oversample.** σ-stop fired with 28 games at 10+0.1 vs 0 at 20+0.2. If the σ-stop had been delayed to game ~150 (where the sampler asymptotes to uniform), the converged number would have shifted upward toward the slower-TC anchors (since clawfish scored higher at slow TC). Estimate of the bias: with effective TC ≈ 25+0.25 and per-TC scores 7/28 (10+0.1), 9/14 (40+0.4), 6.5/14 (60+0.6), the score-weighted average matches the observed 41.96% within noise. A balanced mixed-TC run would shift the converged estimate up by perhaps +50–100 Elo.

**3. Initial estimate 2700 was reasonable.** The K-update walked downward from 2700 to 2601 over 56 games. The walk magnitude (~99 Elo) at avg K ~25 over 56 games means each game contributed ~K/2 / typical-error = ~1.8 Elo of correction toward truth. The trajectory was monotone in the σ-stop window. No M3.F-style "trapped at biased σ-stop" pathology.

**4. The pure-logistic SPRT result (+400 Elo) does NOT translate 1:1 to the UCI_Elo space (+300 at fast-TC).** Stockfish's UCI_LimitStrength has a non-linear strength curve in 2200–2400 (per the M4.D doc); both engines' ratings are not on the same scale, and clawfish-vs-clawfish SPRT logistic Elo is a tight measurement of relative strength but doesn't transfer additively to UCI_Elo space. The per-TC table above is the load-bearing readout.
