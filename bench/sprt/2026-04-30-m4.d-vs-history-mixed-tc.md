# M4.D mixed-TC SPRT vs `M4.C`

Date: 2026-04-30. M4.D's mixed-TC strength gate, queued in `bench/sprt/2026-04-30-m4.d-vs-history.md` and the M4.D milestone retrospective. First consumer of ELOH.D's `--tc-sample` per-pair sampler. Run kicked off in-process (in `elo-iterate`) rather than via `scripts/sprt.sh sprt` because fastchess does not implement per-pair TC sampling — the redefined "draw TC from D, then play standard chess at that TC" framing is an ELOH.D harness affordance.

**Verdict: HEAD significantly stronger than BASELINE on the mixed game.** Δ Elo **+41.89 [+18.18, +65.61]** (pentanomial 95% CI), 138-90-172 in 400 games / 200 pairs, score 56.00%. CI excludes zero by 18 Elo on the lower side. Slow-TC (60+0.6) bucket is individually neutral with wide CI (±72.6 Elo); fast-TC (10+0.1) and 40+0.4 buckets carry the signal. Result is consistent with the existing fast-TC-only SPRT (Δ +65.92 ± 26.52 Elo at tc=10+0.1, run 3 of `bench/sprt/2026-04-30-m4.d-vs-history.md`); aggregating slower TCs with weaker-individually-significant signal pulls the mixed-game point estimate down to +42 Elo.

## Sampler distribution check

ELOH.D's per-pair sampler is supposed to draw uniformly from a 4-bucket distribution. With 200 pairs and a uniform 4-bucket spec, the expected count per bucket is 50 pairs (= 100 games). Observed:

| TC | Expected pairs | Observed pairs | Observed games | χ² contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 50 | 59 | 118 | 1.62 |
| 20+0.2 | 50 | 52 | 104 | 0.08 |
| 40+0.4 | 50 | 45 | 90  | 0.50 |
| 60+0.6 | 50 | 44 | 88  | 0.72 |

χ² = 2.92 for 3 d.o.f.; p-value ≈ 0.40. **Sampler is consistent with uniform** at α=0.05 (critical value 7.815). The slight under-sampling of slow TCs (44 + 45 vs 50 expected) is within the normal sampler-variance band.

## Interpretation

- **The aggregate is significantly positive**: Δ +41.89 with pentanomial 95% CI excluding zero. HEAD wins the mixed game at p < 0.001.
- **Slower-TC sub-result is neutral with wide CI** (60+0.6: 24-24-40, Δ 0.0 ± 72.6). Two interpretations are consistent with the data:
  1. *Genuine TC-dependence*: aspiration windows lose effectiveness at the deepest searches (where ordering is already excellent and the first-try success rate approaches saturation, leaving fewer cycles for re-search amortization to recoup). The depth-amplification thesis from M4.B/M4.C precedent does NOT apply uniformly to aspiration-style pruning.
  2. *Sampling noise*: 88 games at 60+0.6 has 95% CI of ±73 Elo at score=50%. The data is consistent with anywhere from −72 to +72 Elo at this TC; +0.0 is the point estimate but within ±50 Elo there is essentially no power to distinguish.
- **The fast-TC point estimate** (+59.5 Elo at 10+0.1) is consistent with run 3 of `bench/sprt/2026-04-30-m4.d-vs-history.md` (+65.92 ± 26.52 Elo) within ~1σ. Cross-validates the M4.D landing configuration on independent sampling.
- **Decisive-favorable pair ratio of 1.71** (HEAD-favoring decisive pairs / BASELINE-favoring decisive pairs) is below the fast-TC-only run-3 result of 2.10, again consistent with the aggregated slow-TC games tilting closer to neutral.

The TC mismatch (anchor at tc=60+0.6, this measurement is mixed) introduces additional noise; for a tighter HEAD anchor we'd want a fixed-tc=60+0.6 SPRT, which the per-bucket data here cannot provide at the available N=88. The mixed-game number is the headline.
