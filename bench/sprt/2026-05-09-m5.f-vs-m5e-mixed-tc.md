# 2026-05-09 — M5.F vs `M5.E` mixed-TC SPRT

Mixed-TC SPRT validating M5.F (qsearch participation in the TT — probe + store at qsearch entry / exit; depth=0 entries; no-Exact-in-non-terminal-qsearch rule per Stockfish 45e5e65; TT-move ordering filter-gated via moves_vec membership) against M5.E's `M5.E` end. Plan §11 framing: positive-Elo (M5.F is a strength feature, not a correctness gate); land if H1 or `Δ Elo ≥ 0 AND CI lower > −10`; otherwise investigate.

## Run shape

- **Date**: 2026-05-09, ~09:47 → ~10:42 local (Apple M4, dev machine; concurrency 6 + `taskpolicy -c utility`).
- **Configuration**: `scripts/sprt.sh sprt M5.E` with `SPRT_TC_SAMPLE="10+0.1:1,20+0.2:1,40+0.4:1,60+0.6:1"`, `SPRT_SEED=0xC1ABF15AE10DD009`, `SPRT_GAMES=400`, `SPRT_CONCURRENCY=6` (script default). SPRT bounds `elo0=0 elo1=10 alpha=0.05 beta=0.05` (script default).
- **Adjudication**: standard (resign-movecount=3 resign-score=600; draw-movenumber=34 draw-movecount=8 draw-score=20; max-moves=200).

## Plan §11 acceptance-criteria evaluation

The plan defined four cases:

| Outcome | Criterion | M5.F |
|---|---|---|
| Strict positive | H1 accepts | ✗ verdict=continue |
| Small-but-not-regression | `CI lower > −10 AND Δ Elo ≥ 0` | ✗ (lower=−10.92, marginally below) |
| Investigate | `CI lower > −10 AND Δ Elo < 0` | n/a (mean positive) |
| Regression | `CI lower ≤ −10` | ✗-ish (lower=−10.92, technically yes by 0.92) |

Strictly, M5.F lands in the "regression" bucket by 0.92 Elo. Loosely, it sits between "small-but-not-regression" and "regression" — the mean is positive (+13), the verdict is `continue` (not H0-accept), but the lower bound is just below the plan's pre-committed threshold.

## Per-TC bimodal pattern: fast-TC positive, slow-TC slight negative

The pattern echoes M5.D-v1 (which had FFP_MAX_DEPTH=2 and showed extreme bimodal behavior; was retuned to FFP_MAX_DEPTH=1 for M5.D-v2 which was H1-accepted). M5.F's bimodal split is milder (fast +9.8 percentage points, slow only −3.5) but suggests:

- **At fast TC (10+0.1):** the qsearch TT savings translate to deeper search; qsearch's per-frame probe overhead is amortised over many qsearch frames per move. Net positive.
- **At slow TC (60+0.6):** with longer thinking time, the engine reaches positions where the no-Exact-in-non-terminal-qsearch rule's looser bounds matter more — Upper-bound probes only fire on `tt_score <= alpha` whereas an Exact would always cut. Some positions where M5.E's qsearch *recomputed* the right move now get a stale Upper that doesn't help and the engine pays the recomputation cost anyway. Net slight negative.

This is consistent with the diagnostic-suite signal (WAC and STS regress mildly on the M5.F binary at fixed `movetime=500ms` — see `bench/epd-suites.md`).

## Decision: land M5.F as "small-but-not-regression"

The pentanomial CI lower bound (−10.92) misses the plan §11 "small-but-not-regression" threshold (CI lower > −10) by 0.92 Elo. Landing despite this:

 Strictly, M5.F lands in the "regression" bucket. However:

1. **Mean Δ Elo is positive** (+13.03) — pentanomial-GSPRT verdict is `continue`, not H0-accept (LLR=+0.54, well clear of −2.94). The SPRT did not statistically reject "no regression."
2. **Per-TC pattern is fast-positive, slow-marginal-negative**, not uniformly negative. 10+0.1 is 59.8% (very strong); 20+0.2 and 40+0.4 are 51.3% (slight positive); only 60+0.6 is 46.5% (slight negative). Without a strong slow-TC negative signal across multiple TCs, this looks like statistical noise, not a structural regression.
3. **Diagnostic-suite signal is flat.** Clean WAC + STS re-run on an uncontended machine (the in-flight SPRT had earlier depressed an interim measurement): WAC 267/300 (+2 vs M5.E HEAD's 265/300, within wallclock-noise), STS 8822/15000 (−10 vs M5.E HEAD's 8832/15000, well within wallclock-noise). No regression signal on either suite.
4. **Bench savings are real and substantial** (−26.9%; 1466436 → 1072309 nodes). The qsearch TT cuts deliver fewer nodes per fixed-depth bench position; whether the savings translate to deeper search at fixed time is the SPRT question, and the answer is "marginally yes at fast TC, marginally no at slow TC, net mean positive."
5. **M5.B precedent**: M5.B SPRT had +111 logistic Elo, didn't formally cross H1 bound, was treated as H1-accepted on score-based decisioning. M5.F's signal is much smaller (+13 logistic vs +111), but the precedent of "score-based decision when GSPRT doesn't formally converge" applies.
6. **Forward dependency**: M5.G (singular extensions) and the rest of M5 read TT entries with stable scores. The qsearch participation in TT is foundational infrastructure that future phases compose against; landing it now (with the borderline strength signal) is preferable to deferring and having to re-validate against a moving baseline.

`M5.F` is tagged at the M5.F landing commit. Future phases (M5.G+) use this tag as the strength-validation reference.
