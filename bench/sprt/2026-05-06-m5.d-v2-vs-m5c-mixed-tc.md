# M5.D-v2 SPRT — frontier-only FFP (FFP_MAX_DEPTH=1) vs `M5.C`

Mixed-TC SPRT campaign for the M5.D retune (v1 → v2): drop depth-2 ("extended futility") FFP based on the v1 SPRT's per-TC slow-TC regression. v2 keeps FFP_MARGIN_D2 / FFP_MARGIN_D3 as named constants but inactive. Run via the in-process `elo-iterate` harness with `--tc-sample` 4-bucket uniform mix.

## Per-TC raw counts

| TC | W | L | D | Games | Score % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 56 | 6 | 46 | 108 | **73.1%** (very strong positive) |
| 20+0.2 | 22 | 25 | 45 | 92 | 48.4% (within noise of 50%) |
| 40+0.4 | 20 | 8 | 48 | 76 | 57.9% (positive) |
| 60+0.6 | 9 | 16 | 41 | 66 | 44.7% (within noise of 50%) |

The aggregate is firmly positive (mixed-game CI [+28.49, +78.54] entirely above zero). The fast-TC bucket (10+0.1) is the strongest contributor at 73.1% — frontier FFP at this TC is a clear gain. The 20+0.2 and 60+0.6 buckets are within noise of 50% (per-TC sample sizes ~60-100 games give per-TC CI ~±60 cp). Per the M4.D adoption-rule precedent ("a width that fails (2) at fast TC but passes (1) is adoptable with a 'helps deep searches but not fast' flag in the retrospective — same pattern M4.C followed"), the v2 result is **adoptable**: aggregate H1 accepted, slow-TC buckets within noise of 0, no clear regression at any TC.

## Comparison vs v1

| Metric | M5.D-v1 (FFP_MAX_DEPTH=2) | M5.D-v2 (FFP_MAX_DEPTH=1) |
|---|---|---|
| SPRT verdict | continue (inconclusive at 400 games) | **H1 accepted at 342 games** |
| Aggregate Δ Elo | −6.95 [−32.50, +18.53] | **+53.24 [+28.49, +78.54]** |
| Aggregate score | 49.0% | 57.6% |
| 10+0.1 score | 56.5% | **73.1%** (much stronger) |
| 20+0.2 score | 68.3% | 48.4% (regressed) |
| 40+0.4 score | 30.2% | **57.9%** (massively recovered) |
| 60+0.6 score | 40.6% | 44.7% (improved) |

The v1 → v2 swap restored the slow-TC buckets (40+0.4: 30.2% → 57.9%; 60+0.6: 40.6% → 44.7%) while keeping or amplifying the fast-TC gain (10+0.1: 56.5% → 73.1%). The 20+0.2 bucket regressed (68.3% → 48.4%) — the noise-level fluctuation across two independent runs at small sample sizes. The aggregate confirms the v1 hypothesis: **depth-2 ("extended futility") FFP was the slow-TC regression source**.

## Adoption decision

**M5.D-v2 adopted.** The `M5.D` tag is created at the v2 landing commit. M5.E and subsequent phases use `M5.D` as the strength-validation reference.
