## Interpretation — vs the SPRT signal

The v2 SPRT vs `M5.C` showed **+53.24 Elo** with 73.1% score at 10+0.1 (very strong fast-TC gain). This rating-estimate vs Stockfish-2657 shows **−15.65 Elo** with 38.4% score at 10+0.1 (slight fast-TC regression relative to the Stockfish anchor).

These two measurements **use different opponents** and can disagree without impeaching either:

- **The SPRT** is clawfish-vs-clawfish, where both engines share the same eval architecture, time-management, and tactical/strategic profile. The SPRT confirms v2 is genuinely stronger than `M5.C` (the formal validation gate).
- **The rating estimate** is clawfish-vs-Stockfish-UCI_LimitStrength, where Stockfish at UCI_Elo=2657 plays a fundamentally different style (Stockfish's strength-throttling mechanism injects noise / suboptimal moves but preserves Stockfish's strategic understanding, which is much deeper than clawfish's classical PSQT eval). The cross-engine match therefore measures something orthogonal to "is v2 stronger than M5.C?".

The CI overlap with the M5.C anchor (2657 ± 16.49) means M5.D-v2 lands at roughly the same Stockfish-anchored Elo as M5.C — about 2641 ± 43 — even though v2 is +53 Elo over M5.C in head-to-head. The discrepancy is consistent with prior phases: rating-estimate values move slowly across phases because the cross-engine signal is dominated by the gap between clawfish's (classical PSQT) and Stockfish's (NNUE) strategic understanding, not by clawfish's incremental search improvements.

## Adoption decision

The SPRT is the load-bearing validation. v2 is adopted via the +53.24 Elo SPRT signal vs `M5.C`. The rating estimate is recorded for cross-engine context; it does NOT change the adoption decision.
