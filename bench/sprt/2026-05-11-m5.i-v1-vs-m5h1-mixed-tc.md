## Mid-SPRT bimodal noise — false signal

At an early checkpoint (168 games):
- 10+0.1: 55.9% (+41 logistic)
- 20+0.2: 40.0% (−71 logistic)
- 40+0.4: 48.6% (−10)
- 60+0.6: 47.1% (−20)

This "fast-TC wins, mid-TC catastrophic loss" pattern looked qualitatively similar to the M5.G v1 / M5.H2 bimodal-rejection precedents. Plan §11 outcome 4 ("inconclusive — TC-bimodal") would have been the expected diagnosis.

But the pattern dissipated as games accumulated. At 323 games:
- 10+0.1: 44.9% (−36)
- 20+0.2: 40.1% (−71, still)
- 40+0.4: 54.1% (+29)
- 60+0.6: 51.6% (+11)

At 400 games (final), all per-TC scores converged to **~50% ± noise**. The early "fast-TC benefit" was within sample-variance bands of `±10-15%` at N=34 games. The persistent-looking 20+0.2 ~40% at game 168/323 also recovered to 51.7% by game 400.

**Lesson**: with mixed-TC SPRT at 4 buckets, per-bucket N reaches ~100 only at the full 400-game point. Per-TC reads at <200 games are dominated by Bernoulli noise (CIs of ±10-12% at N=80). Don't make v2-iteration decisions on early per-TC samples.

## Verdict

M5.I v1 is **Elo-neutral within measurement noise**. Mean +1.74 Elo with CI [−22, +25] is statistically indistinguishable from zero. No per-TC bucket shows a measurable effect at full game-count.

The literature's prediction that a third tier might help (CPW Crafty schedule, Meesha staged widening) is not borne out for clawfish at its current strength regime. The published counter-evidence (MadChess −9 Elo from removing a three-tier schedule) is consistent with this null result.

**Decision: do not land v1.** Per plan §11 outcome 2: write no-change retrospective; revert the production code; preserve research note + this SPRT log.

## v2 iteration plan (user-directed, 2026-05-11)

The user's instruction to "find a way to reap the benefits at fast TC without losing at slow" was based on the early-noise 168-game checkpoint. With full data, there is **no per-TC signal to salvage**. v2 iteration motivation is weakened.

Two paths forward:
1. **Accept v1 as Elo-neutral, abandon M5.I entirely.** Document in retrospective as a M5.H2-style deferral. The intermediate tier may help at higher engine strength / deeper typical-TC depth; revisit when those preconditions are met.
2. **Try v2 (depth-gated tier 2) as a speculative iteration.** Hypothesis from the M5.G v1/v2 retune precedent: a tunable that didn't help at first may help with a tuned parameter. `ASPIRATION_INTERMEDIATE_MAX_DEPTH ∈ {8, 10, 12}` sweeps the depth axis. No strong empirical motivation from v1's data (all per-TC near 50%); the motivation is "the v1 implementation is Elo-neutral but theoretically should help; try one tuned variant before deferring."

Decision deferred to post-diagnostic-suite read (WAC + STS + Stockfish Elo).
