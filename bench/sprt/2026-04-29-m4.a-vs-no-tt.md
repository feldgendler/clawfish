## Interpretation

- **+282 Elo at fast TC** is well above any reasonable per-phase SPRT bound. The gain is dominated by node reduction: TT cuts the tree by ~77% at default bench depth (172M → 40M nodes at depth 7), so the same wallclock budget reaches deeper effective search.
- Loss / draw rates (15 + 30 of 182) reflect the wallclock-driven scheduling noise documented in `docs/plans/m4.a.md` §10 — TT does not eliminate the per-game variance; it just shifts the expected score.
- Pentanomial `[0, 3, 13, 25, 50]` shows 50 pairs of 2-0 to HEAD vs 0 pairs of 2-0 to BASELINE — strongly asymmetric, consistent with the +282 point estimate.
