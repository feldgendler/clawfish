## Interpretation — lands by the outcome ladder

This is **not** a clean H1-accept. It lands under the established M5.F /
M5.G-v2 precedent: a `continue@cap` result whose post-hoc CI excludes the
small-but-not-regression floor ships. The bar those set —

- M5.F: +13.03 [−10.92, +37.12] → landed ("small-but-not-regression").
- M5.G-v2: +23.49 [+0.65, +46.53] → landed (outcome-2).

— is cleared with large margin here: **CI lower bound +19.68** is far above
0 and the +5/−10 floors, and the midpoint +45.42 is roughly **2× M5.G-v2**.
It is unambiguously not a regression and a real, no-tuning cheap-Elo gain
over the score-neutral baseline (0 by construction). The
deterministic-bench SPRT-exemption does **not** apply (real strength
change), so the outcome ladder is the governing gate — and it says land.

## Caveat — per-TC depth reversal (M6.F watch-item)

Per-TC: `10+0.1` W72-L17 (strong +), `20+0.2` W32-L33 (flat), `40+0.4`
W36-L23 (+), `60+0.6` W16-L31 (**negative**). Unlike M6.A's depth-robust
eval change (per-TC dominant all 4 buckets), CONN-only's benefit is
fast-TC-concentrated and *reverses* at the slowest bucket. CONN's raw
literature magnitude over-helps shallow move ordering and is over-scaled
for this engine's deeper search; the aggregate **mixed-TC** game (the
ELOH.D gate object) is firmly positive but the slow-TC component is
adverse. M6.F's joint Texel must rescale CONN against the CONN-only
baseline; the depth reversal is direct evidence the literature CONN table
is too large for this engine.
