## Per-TC sample distribution — caveat

The σ-stop fired at game 38, before the pre-materialised SplitMix64 TC-sample sequence had given fully balanced bucket coverage. Same observation as the M5.B rating-estimate (which σ-stopped at game 40 with similar fast-TC over-sampling).

| TC | W | L | D | Total games | Score % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 3 | 8 | 3 | **14** | 32.1% |
| 20+0.2 | 4 | 5 | 3 | **12** | 45.8% |
| 40+0.4 | 2 | 2 | 0 | **4** | 50.0% |
| 60+0.6 | 3 | 1 | 4 | **8** | 62.5% |

The slow-TC amplification (62.5% at 60+0.6, but only 32.1% at 10+0.1) mirrors the M5.C SPRT's per-TC structure: LMR's strength gain compounds with depth. The σ-stop's fast-TC oversampling pulls the converged Elo *down* toward the fast-TC clawfish-vs-Stockfish equilibrium; the converged number is therefore an under-estimate of the slow-TC equilibrium.

## Cross-check vs SPRT

- M5.C SPRT vs M5.B: pentanomial **Δ Elo +145.47 [+100.12, +196.09]** logistic.
- M5.C rating-estimate vs M5.B: **+5 Elo** in UCI_Elo space.

The ~30:1 logistic-to-UCI_Elo ratio at this rating tier is much steeper than the M5.A → M5.B ratio (~2.2:1). The most likely explanations:

1. **Fast-TC oversampling bias.** The σ-stop fired with the 10+0.1 bucket dominating; M5.C is much weaker than M5.B at fast TC (because LMR overhead per node hurts more when there are fewer total nodes searched). Re-running with a fixed `--max-games 200` instead of σ-stop would give a per-TC-balanced picture closer to the slow-TC equilibrium.
2. **Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength's reduction mechanism saturates** as clawfish gets stronger; per the M4.D rating-estimate doc Observation §5, UCI_Elo numbers are not directly comparable across TCs because the reduction strategy interacts with TC differently than full Stockfish.
3. **Pure noise.** σ at 16.49 means the +5 Elo number is ~0.3σ away from "no change vs M5.B". Statistically indistinguishable from zero in this rating-estimate setup.

The SPRT remains the strength gate. The rating-estimate is informational anchoring; the +5 Elo number should be read as "M5.C is at least as strong as M5.B against Stockfish, with the slow-TC signal positive and the σ-stop biasing toward the fast-TC under-performance."
