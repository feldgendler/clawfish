## Why this run

The M4.D-end strength reported in the README has been a chained estimate (M3.F's ~2114 anchor + sum of clawfish-vs-clawfish SPRT Δs through M4.A–D) since M3 closed. The chain was acknowledged as approximate (±~160 Elo combined uncertainty) but had not been validated by direct measurement against an external opponent at any post-M3 milestone. With ELOH.B/C/D in tree, a per-TC direct measurement is now cheap.

## Method

**Harness:** in-process ELOH (`src/bin/elo-iterate.rs`).

**Per-TC iteration parameters** (after a methodology pass — see "Methodology notes" below):
- `--initial-elo` chosen close to the expected parity point.
- `--k0 40 --tau 30` (or `--tau 50` for tc=20+0.2 r2).
- `--target-sigma 20 --stop-window 40 --stop-window-confirm 10` (target-sigma 15 / window 50 / confirm 15 on the tc=20+0.2 retry).
- `--concurrency 4`, `--virtual-clock`, `--engine-launch-prefix 'taskpolicy -c utility'` on both engines.
- `--opponent-option UCI_LimitStrength=true` (the harness drives Stockfish's `UCI_Elo` from the running estimate after each game).
- Stockfish 18 (`/opt/homebrew/bin/stockfish`, Apple Silicon binary).

**Hardware:** Apple M4 base (4 P + 6 E cores). Both engines under `taskpolicy -c utility` QoS. Virtual-clock enabled — clawfish measures search time in `CLOCK_THREAD_CPUTIME_ID` (per ADR-0021); Stockfish does not advertise the option, so its TC remains wallclock (asymmetric mitigation, documented as a known limitation).

## Trajectories

### tc=10+0.1
First attempt with initial=2950 (mistakenly extrapolated from the ~2962 chained estimate) hit a Robbins-Monro pathology: the estimate walked from 2950 down to 2649.92 over 64 games at score 8.6%, but K decayed with τ=10 before the random walk reached truth, and σ-stopping fired falsely on the stable-but-biased estimate. Discarded.

Retry with initial=2290 (extrapolated from the failed run's score) and τ=30 converged cleanly: 54 games, 24-22-8, σ=11.86 at the final estimate of 2300.01. Score 51.85% — exactly at parity within noise.

### tc=20+0.2
Run 1, initial=2300, τ=30: walked up from 2300 to 2466.21 in 64 games, σ=7.87 at convergence, W-L-D 35-20-9 (61.72%). The high score across the run is consistent with the trajectory bias — average opponent UCI_Elo across the 64 games was ~2380, predicting a 62.1% score for clawfish=2466 (observed 61.72%, within 0.5%). The converged estimate is internally consistent.

Run 2, initial=2540, τ=50, target-sigma=15: ran out to 122 games without firing σ-stop (estimate wobbled in the 2410-2480 range; σ stayed in the 15-30 band because slower K decay kept the per-game updates large). Stopped manually at game 122; running estimate 2421.58, score-derived clawfish ~2444 at avg opp ~2475. The two readings bracket parity at ~2440-2466; the run-1 estimate 2466 is taken as the tc=20+0.2 point estimate for this milestone.

### tc=40+0.4
Initial=2570, τ=30: walked down from 2570 to 2531.45 in 78 games. σ=9.17 at convergence. W-L-D 32-36-10 (47.44%). At the converged estimate against avg opp ~2550 across the trajectory, expected score 47.3% (observed 47.4%). Consistent.

### tc=60+0.6
Initial=2575, τ=30: walked down from 2575 to 2483.42 in 70 games. σ=12.51 at convergence. W-L-D 26-34-10 (42.86%). At the converged estimate against avg opp ~2530 across the trajectory, expected score 43.3% (observed 42.86%). Consistent.

## Observations

**1. The chained estimate of ~2962 mixed-TC was substantially too high.** Direct measurements land at 2300-2531 across the same four TCs. The discrepancy is on the order of 400-600 Elo — far beyond the README's previously-reported ±~160 combined uncertainty.

**2. The probable cause is Stockfish UCI_Elo non-linearity.** The original M3.F anchor write-up (`2026-04-29-online-elo-iteration.md`) flagged a "sharp transition zone in 2200-2400" in Stockfish's UCI_Elo curve. Our M3.F anchor at ~2114 sat below that zone; by M4.D the chain crossed it. Within Stockfish's transition zone, equal nominal-Elo increments do not correspond to equal strength steps, so chaining clawfish-vs-clawfish Δ-SPRTs on top of an anchor below the zone compounds calibration error.

**3. The within-project Δ-SPRTs remain trustworthy as relative measurements.** M4.A's +282, M4.B's +488, M4.C's +36, M4.D's +42 are clawfish-vs-clawfish at fixed-baseline; they don't depend on Stockfish's UCI_Elo calibration. The "absolute" interpretation of their sum is what fails.

**4. The TC-to-Elo curve is not monotonic at the slow end** (60+0.6 < 40+0.4 by ~48 Elo, within combined ~2σ noise but unexpected). One plausible explanation: Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength's reduction mechanism saturates with TC differently than full Stockfish — at slow TC its limit may bind less strictly, narrowing the relative gap to clawfish. Noise is also a defensible explanation given the 70-game sample.

**5. Per-TC numbers are not directly comparable across TCs** because the opponent's effective strength under UCI_LimitStrength shifts with TC. Each row is "clawfish's Elo on Stockfish-UCI_LimitStrength-at-this-TC." A single mixed-TC number obscures this and is not reported here.

## Methodology notes

The first tc=10+0.1 attempt hit a known Robbins-Monro pathology: with K0=40, τ=10, and an initial estimate 750 Elo above truth, K decayed to ~5 long before the random walk reached parity. The σ-stopping criterion (sample stddev of trailing 30 estimates) measures *estimate stability*, not *truth proximity*; with K small and the walk almost stalled, σ falsely dropped below threshold while the estimate was still ~350 Elo above truth.

Three guardrails for future runs:
- Start the estimate close to truth. The Δ to truth, divided by K0/2, is the rough number of games burnt walking through the offset; with K0=40 that's ~2 games per Elo of offset before K decays.
- Use τ ≥ 30 (not the default τ=10). At τ=10 K decays below 5 by game 70; at τ=30 it stays above 10 through game 200, keeping the estimate responsive while still narrowing.
- Be willing to interpret a "biased-but-stable" σ-stop as a false positive — sanity-check the converged estimate by computing trajectory-aware expected score and comparing to observed.

`scripts/elo-iterate.sh`'s defaults (initial=2171, τ=10, target-sigma=30) work for M3-era clawfish near a 2114 anchor; they don't generalize to a milestone whose true rating is unknown by ±100 Elo. A future tooling change should make these defaults less footgun-prone.

## Caveats

- **Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength is not a fixed scale across TCs.** Numbers are not interchangeable.
- **Sample size is small** — 54-78 games per TC. Per-TC σ ~10-13 Elo from the harness; true CI including model uncertainty is wider.
- **Asymmetric virtual-clock.** Clawfish runs on CPU-time TC; Stockfish on wallclock. Under thermal load, clawfish gets relatively more search than Stockfish at the same nominal TC. Mitigated by P-core utility QoS but not eliminated.
- **The discarded first tc=10+0.1 attempt is preserved** at `target/elo-iterate/run-tc10-20260501T022321/` for postmortem.
