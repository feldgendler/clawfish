## Interpretation

- **+487 Elo at fast TC** is even higher than M4.A's +282 Elo gain over no-TT. The size is initially surprising (the literature for killer moves typically reports +30–60 Elo against engines that already have history + aspiration + SEE), but on this engine the size is plausible because:
  - **Killer is the first quiet-ordering technique layered on M4.A.** Without history, all non-killer quiets sort by movegen order at score 0; killer ordering re-prioritizes 1–2 quiets per ply that are highly likely to refute. Marginal Elo from the *first* quiet-ordering technique is much larger than the marginal Elo of subsequent ones (history at M4.C will diminish).
  - **Short TC (10+0.1)** amplifies ordering improvements: better cutoffs → deeper effective search at fixed wallclock. Bench: 44% node reduction (39.96M → 22.24M nodes at default depth 7).
  - **Adjudication threshold `score=600`** catches small advantages early, converting "M4.B finds the tactic 1–2 plies deeper" into "baseline resigns on move 30" rather than "endgame technique decides at move 80".
  - **Engine is at the lower-Elo end where ordering dominates.** At 3000+ Elo with fully-ordered move lists, killer's marginal value is small; at our ~2400 Elo (M4.A end) with TT-only ordering, the first quiet-ordering boost is large.
- **Pentanomial `[0, 0, 8, 2, 69]`**: 69 pairs went 2-0 to HEAD vs 0 pairs to BASELINE. 8 pairs went 0.5-1.5 (the only games BASELINE won were as White scoring a single point). No 0-2-to-BASELINE pair at all. Confirms the asymmetry isn't draw-statistics noise.
- **WL/DD Ratio = inf**: zero "double-draw" pairs. Most games are decisive, consistent with the adjudication threshold catching tactics early. Compare to M4.A's 14.29% draw rate — TT-only games drew more often because both sides had similar tree-pruning quality; killer ordering creates enough asymmetry that stalemate pairs don't form.

The point estimate (+487) carries substantial uncertainty (±125 Elo at 95%), so the "true" delta could be anywhere in [+362, +613]. Cross-validation when M4.C lands will measure killer's marginal gain against a baseline with history (`M4.C`) — that's where the +30–60 literature number should match.
