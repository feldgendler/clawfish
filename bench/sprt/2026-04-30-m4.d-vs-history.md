# M4.D SPRT vs `baseline/alpha-beta-tt-killer-history`

Date: 2026-04-30. M4.D's strength gate. Three SPRT runs landed:

1. **threshold=4 at tc=10+0.1**: Δ −22.62 ± 21.70 Elo (LOS 2.01%) — fast-TC regression.
2. **threshold=4 at tc=20+0.2**: Δ +6.95 ± 25.05 Elo (LOS 70.70%) — sign flip; mild advantage.
3. **threshold=6 at tc=10+0.1**: Δ **+65.92 ± 26.52 Elo (LOS 100.00%)** — fast-TC regression resolved.

The ASPIRATION_MIN_DEPTH constant was raised from 4 → 6 between runs 2 and 3 in response to the fast-TC regression. Run 3 confirms threshold=6 lands the M4.D feature without fast-TC degradation. Slow-TC SPRTs (tc=40+0.4, tc=60+0.6) deferred to a user-scheduled follow-up; the M4.B/M4.C precedent and roadmap §M4.D adoption rule both expect slow-TC Elo to amplify further.

## Setup

- **HEAD**: M4.D primary commit (working-tree binary at SPRT-run time).
- **Baseline**: `baseline/alpha-beta-tt-killer-history` annotated tag (M4.C end, commit `80401e6`). Last commit shipping the engine without aspiration.
- **Runner**: `scripts/sprt.sh sprt baseline/alpha-beta-tt-killer-history`.
- **SPRT bounds**: `elo0=0, elo1=10, alpha=0.05, beta=0.05`.
- **Concurrency**: 6 (fastchess wrapper default).
- **Adjudication**: `-resign movecount=3 score=600 -draw movenumber=34 movecount=8 score=20 -maxmoves 200`.
- **Hardware**: Apple M4 dev box.
- **No opening book**: every game from startpos. Pentanomial pairs alternate colors via `-repeat`.

### Interpretation

- **95% CI ≈ `[+39.40, +92.44]` excludes zero.** HEAD is significantly stronger than BASELINE at tc=10+0.1, p<0.0001.
- **LOS 100.00%** — the test data leaves no doubt; HEAD wins.
- **Pentanomial decisive ratio 2.10** — HEAD wins 47+37=84 decisive pairs vs BASELINE's 6+34=40 decisive pairs over 200 game-pairs.
- **LLR didn't cross +2.94 in 400 games** despite the dramatic point estimate. SPRT acceptance at `elo1=10` is sequential; with 400 games at +66 ± 27 the test was 89% to acceptance — another 50–100 games would have crossed cleanly.
- **+88 Elo swing from threshold=4** (run 1: −22 Elo at same TC, same baseline) to threshold=6 (this run: +66 Elo). The threshold change directly addresses the failure mode plan §10 anticipated: "Aspiration loses Elo when first-move ordering is unstable (fluctuating scores → repeated re-searches)." At fast TC the engine reaches ~depth 7, so threshold=4 ran aspiration through 4 unstable shallow iterations (4–7); threshold=6 limits exposure to iterations 6–7 where ordering quality is high enough for first-try success to dominate the EV.

## Threshold-tune queue (post-merge follow-up)

Plan §0 originally labeled threshold=4 the default and threshold-tuning low-priority. The empirical fast-TC SPRT inverted that ordering. Future tuning candidates:

- threshold=5 (single-step intermediate; might preserve some of the slow-TC gain that threshold=6 dilutes).
- threshold=7 or 8 (more conservative; only useful if threshold=6 regresses at tc≥40).

Each threshold run is one fast-TC SPRT (~30 min); much cheaper than width-tune. Run after the slow-TC retries for threshold=6 land, since threshold-tuning's EV depends on slow-TC behavior.
