# 2026-05-10 — M5.G v2 mixed-TC rating estimate vs Stockfish UCI_Elo=2641

Mixed-TC rating estimate of the M5.G v2-landed binary (`SE_MIN_DEPTH=6`, `SE_MARGIN_PER_DEPTH=1`, `SE_TT_DEPTH_DELTA=3`) against Stockfish 18 with `UCI_LimitStrength=true UCI_Elo=2641`. The 2641 anchor is M5.D-v2's Stockfish reference, carried through M5.E (~2622 ± 45) and M5.F (~2636 ± 43); the goal is to confirm M5.G v2 stays within the same statistical band.

## Run shape

- **Date**: 2026-05-10 14:21 → 15:49 local (Apple M4 Max; concurrency 4 + `taskpolicy -c utility`).
- **Configuration**: `scripts/sprt.sh rating-estimate` with `SPRT_TC_SAMPLE="10+0.1:1,20+0.2:1,40+0.4:1,60+0.6:1"`, `SPRT_SEED=0xC1ABF15AE10DD00E`, `SPRT_GAMES=200`, `SPRT_CONCURRENCY=4`, `STOCKFISH_ELO=2641`. Frozen-K + disabled-σ-stop (per `scripts/sprt.sh rating-estimate` defaults).
- **Adjudication**: standard (resign-movecount=3 resign-score=600; draw-movenumber=34 draw-movecount=8 draw-score=20; max-moves=200).
- **CPU-contention caveat**: this run was launched while another rating-estimate (`20260510T141046-stockfish-2641-rating-estimate`, the orchestrator's first attempt) was still running. Both rating-estimates competed for CPU throughout this run's 90-min wallclock (~2× expected solo runtime). The qualitative result (CI straddling zero — HEAD ≈ Stockfish-2641 within noise) is robust to contention since both engines pay the same wallclock penalty under `taskpolicy -c utility`, but the precise per-TC breakdown should be considered approximate.

## Independent confirmation run

A parallel rating-estimate `20260510T141046-stockfish-2641-rating-estimate` started 11 min before this one and ran for 90 min (also CPU-contended throughout; finished 15:40 local) reported:

```
ci: elo=-6.95 [-51.16, +37.04] pairs=100
```

Mean −6.95 vs this run's +3.47; both CIs straddle zero. The 10-Elo difference between the two runs is within sampling variance for 200-game rating estimates against Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength (which has its own internal stochasticity from `Skill Level` randomization).

## Rating estimate

Combining both 200-game runs (400 games total) into an informal estimate: mean Δ Elo ≈ −1.7 (average of −6.95 and +3.47), suggesting M5.G v2 rates at approximately **~2639 ± 44 Elo** on the Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength scale — statistically indistinguishable from M5.F's 2636 ± 43, M5.E's 2622 ± 45, and M5.D-v2's 2641. The four estimates' CIs all overlap; within rating-estimate noise, M5.D-v2 → M5.E → M5.F → M5.G is **flat on the Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength scale**.

This is consistent with the in-engine M5.G-vs-M5.F SPRT result of Δ Elo +23.49 [+0.65, +46.53]: SPRT measures clawfish-vs-clawfish strength tightly (the engines share ordering quality, search shape, and TT semantics so the deltas are directly attributable), but UCI_LimitStrength is a non-linear scale at 2200–2700 (per the M4.D + M5.A + M5.B + M5.C rating-estimate docs) and chained logistic-Elo gains do not translate 1:1 to UCI_Elo space.
