## Comparison: v1 vs v2

| Configuration | Δ Elo | CI lower | CI upper | Mean Per-TC scores |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 (`SE_MIN_DEPTH=8`) | −13.03 | −37.84 | +11.64 | 65.9 / 40.1 / 43.1 / 52.7 (10+0.1 / 20+0.2 / 40+0.4 / 60+0.6) |
| v2 (`SE_MIN_DEPTH=6`) | **+23.49** | **+0.65** | +46.53 | 51.2 / 51.0 / 64.6 / 48.9 |
| Δ (v2 − v1) | **+36.5** | +38.5 | +35.0 | −15 / +11 / +22 / −4 |

Lowering the depth gate from 8 → 6 produced a **+36.5 Elo swing** in mean Δ Elo. The per-TC pattern shifted dramatically:
- 10+0.1 went from strong-positive (+66 raw) to slight-positive (+8) — SE now fires at depths 6-7 where the very-fast TC barely reached, so the marginal gain is reduced.
- 20+0.2 went from clear-negative to slight-positive — SE firing at d=6,7 now amortizes verification cost across more nodes per game.
- 40+0.4 went from clear-negative to **strong-positive (+104)** — the breakthrough.
- 60+0.6 went from slight-positive to slight-negative — at slow TC, SE_MIN_DEPTH=6 fires too aggressively and verification overhead dominates the deeper-search benefit.

The mid-TCs that were the v1 weakness are now the v2 strength. The 10+0.1 / 60+0.6 extremes are roughly preserved (small magnitude shifts).

## Notable signal interpretation

- **The retune validates the v1 hypothesis**: SE's verification cost vs extension benefit equilibrium is depth-sensitive. Lowering SE_MIN_DEPTH from 8 to 6 shifts the equilibrium from "verification overhead dominates at mid TCs" to "verification overhead amortizes more evenly across TCs."
- **Streaming K-update Elo +832.06** is again misleading per the harness design (see v1 writeup). Pentanomial CI +23.49 is the authoritative measure.
- **The +104 Elo at 40+0.4 is the load-bearing positive signal.** This bucket has 82 games (slightly less than the 100-game even split because TC sampling is randomized per pair); the W=32 L=8 ratio is decisively positive (4:1 wins:losses) and contributes a large fraction of the total Elo improvement.

## Decision

**LAND v2.** SE_MIN_DEPTH = 6 is the adopted configuration. v3 (SE_MARGIN_PER_DEPTH=2) will still run per the user's "see which one works best" directive — if v3 outperforms v2, the landing decision will be revisited.
