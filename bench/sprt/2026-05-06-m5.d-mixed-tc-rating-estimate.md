## Per-TC raw counts

| TC | W | L | D | Games | Score % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 16 | 17 | 11 | 44 | 48.9% |

| 40+0.4 | 19 | 24 | 9 | 52 | 45.2% |
| 60+0.6 | 25 | 16 | 13 | 54 | **58.3%** |

The per-TC pattern here is less clearly bimodal than the M5.D-vs-M5.C SPRT (where slow-TC scored 30–41%). The 60+0.6 bucket here is **positive** (58.3% vs Stockfish-2657), while in the SPRT it was negative (40.6% vs M5.C-baseline). Two readings:

1. **Stockfish at UCI_Elo=2657 may not represent the same rating curve at 60+0.6 as at 10+0.1.** Stockfish UCI_LimitStrength is calibrated by Stockfish at slower TC than 60+0.6; at 60+0.6 the Stockfish-2657 setting may itself be playing weaker, which masks any clawfish slow-TC regression that the M5.C-baseline SPRT clearly showed. The vs-clawfish-baseline SPRT is the more rigorous comparison; the vs-Stockfish run is a coarse anchor.

2. **CI [−40, +55] is too wide to claim a real change against Stockfish.** The aggregate Δ Elo +6.95 is within the noise envelope; the per-TC swings (44–58%) are within ±10% which corresponds to ±70 Elo at small per-bucket sample sizes (~50 games per TC). M5.D and M5.C are statistically indistinguishable on this anchor.

## Adoption decision

**Estimated Elo: ~2664 ± 48.** Statistically indistinguishable from M5.C's 2657 ± 16.49 anchor. Listed for record-keeping; not a basis for adoption (the M5.C-baseline SPRT is the formal gate, and that came back inconclusive — see [`2026-05-06-m5.d-vs-m5c-mixed-tc.md`](2026-05-06-m5.d-vs-m5c-mixed-tc.md)).
