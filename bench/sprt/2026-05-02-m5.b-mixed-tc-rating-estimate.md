## Per-TC sample distribution — caveat

The σ-stop fired at game 40 (pair 20), before the pre-materialised SplitMix64 TC-sample sequence had given fully balanced bucket coverage:

| TC | W | L | D | Total games | Pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 6 | 9 | 3 | **18** | 9 |
| 20+0.2 | 1 | 3 | 0 | **4** | 2 |
| 40+0.4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **14** | 7 |
| 60+0.6 | 3 | 1 | 0 | **4** | 2 |

Distribution is fast-TC-weighted (10+0.1 dominates with 45% of games; 20+0.2 and 60+0.6 are under-represented at 10% each). Per the M4.D 200-pair calibration, the SplitMix64 sampler is unbiased at scale (chi-squared p ≈ 0.40); short-N early-sample skew is expected to wash out by ~50 pairs. Practical interpretation: the **2652 Elo** estimate represents clawfish at an effective TC ≈ 25+0.25 — fast-TC-weighted, similar to the M5.A rating-estimate's effective TC of ≈ 25+0.25.
