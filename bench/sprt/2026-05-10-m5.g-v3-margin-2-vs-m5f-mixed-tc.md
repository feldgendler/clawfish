The trajectory is decisively negative — score 44.5% sustained across 256 games. Final pentanomial CI at 400 games would be approximately mean ~−40 with half-width ~20 → CI [−60, −20]. **Outcome 4 (H0-equivalent regression)**: do NOT land.

## Decision

**Run aborted as a landing candidate at the v2 SPRT-acceptance point** (2026-05-10 ~04:30 local). The trajectory is unambiguous: v3's "raise the margin" direction is the wrong retune. The run continued to formal termination in the background for documentation completeness; partial data above.

The **v2 retune is the adopted M5.G configuration** (`SE_MIN_DEPTH=6, SE_MARGIN_PER_DEPTH=1`). v3's negative result confirms:
- SE works best when it fires *more* often (lower depth gate, v2), not less often (higher margin, v3).
- The v1 weakness was insufficient firing at mid TCs, not over-firing.
- Verification overhead amortizes well when SE fires across more nodes; making SE more selective concentrates the cost without amortizing.
