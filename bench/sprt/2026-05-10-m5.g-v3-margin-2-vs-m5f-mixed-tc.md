## Decision

**v3 rejected.** The signal magnitude (Δ Elo −46.66, H0-accept verdict) is decisively worse than v1's −13 — making SE *more selective* at the same depth gate produces a stronger regression than v1's literature-default config.

The **v2 retune is the adopted M5.G configuration** (`SE_MIN_DEPTH=6, SE_MARGIN_PER_DEPTH=1`). v3's negative result confirms:
- SE works best when it fires *more* often (lower depth gate, v2), not less often (higher margin, v3).
- The v1 weakness was insufficient firing at mid TCs, not over-firing.
- Verification overhead amortizes well when SE fires across more nodes; making SE more selective concentrates the cost without amortizing.

**Comparison across all three M5.G variants:**

| Variant | Δ Elo (pentanomial CI) | Verdict | Pentanomial | Aggregate score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 (depth=8, margin=1) | −13.03 [−37.84, +11.64] | continue (LLR=−1.14) | [17,51,73,48,11] | 48.1% |
| v2 (depth=6, margin=1) **LANDED** | **+23.49 [+0.65, +46.53]** | continue (LLR=+1.36) | [10,40,71,71,8] | 53.4% |
| v3 (depth=8, margin=2) | **−46.66 [−73.63, −20.25]** | **H0 (LLR=−2.88)** | [28,50,64,43,6] | 43.6% |

The 60-Elo swing from v1 to v2 (−13 → +23) demonstrates lowering the depth gate is the strong-positive direction. The 34-Elo swing from v1 to v3 (−13 → −47) demonstrates raising the margin is the strong-negative direction. Together: the 70-Elo swing from v3 to v2 quantifies how sensitive SE's net Elo is to the firing-rate dial.
