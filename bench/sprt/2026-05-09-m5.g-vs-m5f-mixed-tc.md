## Notable signal interpretation

- **Strong bimodal per-TC pattern.** Very-fast (10+0.1) and slow (60+0.6) TCs net positive; mid TCs (20+0.2 and 40+0.4) net clearly negative. The aggregate Δ Elo is dragged down by the mid-TC band.
- **Hypothesis on the bimodal cause.** SE's verification cost vs extension benefit reaches different equilibria across TCs:
  - At 10+0.1 (very fast): verification's reduced-depth recursion is cheap relative to the extension's depth-budget gain on hot tactical lines; engine reaches deeper effective depth on PV.
  - At 60+0.6 (slow): the engine reaches depths well above SE_MIN_DEPTH=8 on most positions; SE fires often and the deep extensions translate to tactical wins. Verification cost is amortized across the long search.
  - At 20+0.2 / 40+0.4 (mid): the engine reaches depths near SE_MIN_DEPTH=8 marginally; SE fires intermittently; verification cost dominates the shallow extension's benefit. Net negative.
- **STS +214 credit despite SPRT regression.** STS measures HEAD-only tactical accuracy at 1000ms/move (effectively 60+0.6-equivalent). The +214 credit confirms SE is genuinely improving tactical solving at slow/fixed TC. The SPRT regression is therefore not a tactical-correctness issue but a TC-dependent search-economy issue.
- **Streaming K-update Elo (+687) is misleading per harness design.** The Robbins-Monro K-update uses `opp_elo = current_estimate` (`src/elo_iterate/controller.rs:595`), making expected_score = 0.5 always; with K=40 initial decay, early-loaded wins inflate the running estimate while late losses get small K updates. Pentanomial CI is the authoritative measure (per ADR-0022). This run's streaming +687 corresponds to actual pentanomial mean −13.
