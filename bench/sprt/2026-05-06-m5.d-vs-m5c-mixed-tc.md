**This is the OPPOSITE of every prior M5 phase.** M5.A/B/C all showed slow-TC amplification (M5.C: 58.3% at 10+0.1 → 78.9% at 60+0.6). M5.D shows the inverse: strongly positive at fast TC, strongly negative at slow TC.

## Interpretation

Two non-mutually-exclusive hypotheses:

1. **Depth-2 ("extended futility") FFP is the culprit.** Heinz 1998's original frontier-FP formulation was depth-1 only; the depth-2 extension was the same paper but adds risk for unclear gain. At fast TC the engine reaches shallow depths frequently, so depth-1 FFP fires a lot and depth-2 is rarely active deep in the tree. At slow TC the engine reaches deeper search, depth-2 FFP-eligible nodes accumulate further from the root, and pruned moves at depth 2 may be tactically critical positions a deeper search would have refuted.

2. **Sample noise per TC.** ~100 games per TC gives ~±60 Elo CI per bucket. The fast-TC and slow-TC swings, while striking, are within the noise envelope of one bucket against another. The aggregate CI [−32.50, +18.53] overlaps zero, so the signed sign of the mixed-TC delta cannot be claimed with confidence.

Hypothesis (1) is the more credible story given the literature — depth-2 FFP is well-known as the riskier extension. The strong 68.3% score at 20+0.2 is hard to explain as pure noise.

## Adoption decision

**Not adopted as a strength gain.** The mixed-TC SPRT did not formally accept H1, the aggregate score is below 50%, and the per-TC breakdown shows clear bimodal signal (positive fast / negative slow) that violates the "doesn't regress anywhere" floor in the M4.D mixed-TC adoption rule.

`M5.D` tag is **NOT created** at this commit. The M5.D code lands as a documented mixed-result phase; M5.E / future phases use `M5.C` as the strength-validation reference until a future FFP retune (or a different M5 phase) re-establishes a clean strength gain over M5.C.

## Recommended follow-up

`FFP_MAX_DEPTH = 1` retune campaign. Per-TC evidence above strongly implicates depth-2 as the slow-TC regression's source. Frontier-only FFP is the literature-conservative choice (Heinz 1998 original; CPW first formulation). If the retune validates positive across all TCs, land as M5.D-v2 and create `M5.D` then.

Other follow-up candidates (lower priority):
- `FFP_MARGIN_D2 = 100` (down from 150) — tighter margin at depth 2 may reduce tactical blindness without dropping coverage entirely.
- Gives-check exemption — implement `gives_check(pos, mv)` and re-SPRT.
