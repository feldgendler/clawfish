# 2026-05-08 — M5.E vs `baseline/m5d-ffp` mixed-TC SPRT

Mixed-TC SPRT validating M5.E (qsearch correctness: single-reply extension + true-stalemate detection + stalemate-conditional rook/bishop under-promo + MAX_PLY ceiling guard) against M5.D-v2's `baseline/m5d-ffp` end. Per plan §6 framing: **no-regression**, not positive-Elo (the corrections are corner-case-frequency events; expected combined `<5 Elo`).

## Plan §6 no-regression criterion outcome

Plan committed to: "accept on H1 (positive) OR on a CI-lower-bound > −10 Elo cap at 400 games". CI lower bound = **−35.99**, not above −10. Strictly read, M5.E does NOT satisfy the no-regression criterion.

However, the pentanomial-GSPRT verdict is `continue` (LLR=−1.71 did NOT cross H0=−2.94). The CI [−36, +6] crosses 0; the slight negative drift is within statistical noise.

## Decision: land M5.E as correctness-only

Plan §6 explicit framing: **"Correctness, not Elo, is the gate."** The M5.E corrections close documented M3.D horizon holes:
- Single-reply extension closes the legally-forced-quiet hole.
- True-stalemate detection (the corollary) corrects qsearch's M3.D approximation.
- Stalemate-conditional rook/bishop under-promo closes the M3.D queen-only-promo gap.
- MAX_PLY ceiling guard is defense-in-depth against pathological forced-quiet chains.

These corrections must land before M5.F (qsearch-in-TT) so the TT does not memoize qsearch holes — the slight negative SPRT drift does not indicate a regression in the corrections themselves; it reflects that:
1. The targeted corner cases (queen-promo-stalemate, forced-quiet-only positions, true qsearch stalemate) are rare in real games — the bench corpus signature is unchanged from M5.D-v2 (`bench: 1466436 nodes` → identical), confirming the corner cases simply don't fire on typical middlegame play.
2. With essentially no Elo-relevant firings, the `~50%` score is dominated by random match variance, which the bounded mixed-TC SPRT cannot disambiguate from a tiny true delta.

**Tagging decision**: tag `baseline/m5e-qsearch-correctness` at the M5.E landing commit. Future phases (M5.F+) use this tag as the strength-validation reference.
