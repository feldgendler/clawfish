## Interpretation

M6.A is the **first phase to break above the M5.D-v2 → M5.G UCI_Elo plateau** (M5.E ~2622, M5.F ~2636, M5.G ~2639 — all statistically flat within ±44 against this 2641 anchor). The midpoint moves +37 to ~2678; the CI lower bound (~2639) just touches the old plateau, so the break is **directionally positive but not statistically decisive on the UCI scale**.

This is fully consistent with the +250.57 in-engine SPRT delta and is **not** a contradiction. As documented across the M4.D / M5.A–G rating-estimate writeups: clawfish-vs-clawfish GSPRT measures relative strength tightly (engines share ordering quality, search shape, TT semantics — deltas are directly attributable), but `UCI_LimitStrength` is a **non-linear scale at 2200–2700** and Stockfish applies internal node/skill reduction that does not scale 1:1 with mixed-TC wallclock. Chained logistic-Elo gains compress heavily into UCI_Elo space. The +250 internal delta vs `M5.H1` (an MG-only baseline with no EG king PST) translating to ~+37 UCI_Elo is the expected compression, not a measurement failure.

**Curve datapoint:** M5.D-v2 ~2641 → M5.E ~2622 → M5.F ~2636 → M5.G ~2639 → **M6.A ~2678 [2639, 2718]**. The absolute rating curve has its first marginal-but-real uptick after four flat phases.
