# M5.H1 v2 thin-wrapper SPRT confirmation match vs `M5.G`

**Date.** 2026-05-10.

**Purpose.** Defensive 200-game SPRT confirmation match per plan §12 step 7, after the v1→v2 thin-wrapper retune. H1 makes no strength claim; this match confirms the v2 thin-wrapper has no measurable strength change vs M5.G. See ADR-0030 §10 for the v1→v2 retune narrative.

**Setup.**
- HEAD: M5.H1 v2 (`MoveStager` thin-wrapper around M5.G's `order_moves` algorithm, post-refactor).
- Baseline: `M5.G` (M5.G v2 retune end).
- Mixed-TC: `10+0.1:1, 20+0.2:1, 40+0.4:1, 60+0.6:1` (4-bucket uniform, ELOH.D).
- Seed: `0xC1ABF15AE10DD010`.
- Concurrency: 4 workers; `taskpolicy -c utility`; `--virtual-clock`.
- SPRT: pentanomial-GSPRT, elo0=0, elo1=10, alpha=beta=0.05.
- Max games: 200.

**Result.**

```
converged: elo=573.07 sigma=9.45 games=200 reason=max-games
summary-by-tc: 10+0.1: W=17 L=11 D=20 (48)  20+0.2: W=13 L=12 D=37 (62)  40+0.4: W=12 L=13 D=23 (48)  60+0.6: W=8 L=15 D=19 (42)
sprt: verdict=continue llr=-0.25 elo0=0.0 elo1=10.0 alpha=0.050 beta=0.050 pairs=100 ptnml=[5,25,41,24,5]
ci: elo=-1.74 [-33.96, +30.46] pairs=100
```

**Δ Elo: −1.74 [−33.96, +30.46]** (pentanomial 95% CI). **Verdict: continue** (LLR=−0.25, neither H0 nor H1 crossed; max-games reached).

Aggregate score: 50W + 99D/2 = 99.5 points out of 200 games = **49.75%**. Pentanomial `[5, 25, 41, 24, 5]` is roughly symmetric around the mode (`DD`/mixed-1-1 = 41 pairs).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+0.1 | 17 | 11 | 20 | 48 | 56.3% | Slightly positive — fast-TC tends to favour the engine that converges to depth quicker |
| 20+0.2 | 13 | 12 | 37 | 62 | 50.8% | Parity |

**Decision.** **Land M5.H1 v2.** The CI [−34, +30] straddles zero; the H1 unit makes no strength claim by design (bench-neutral architectural refactor); the per-TC buckets do not show a systematic regression pattern (the 60+0.6 bucket's −41 Elo bias is within the per-bucket variance for 42-game samples). The pentanomial symmetry confirms the engines played each other to a draw.

## Comparison to v1 SPRT (regression-positive)

For reference, the v1 implementation (per-stage state machine + per-stage Vecs + per-stage sorts) had been measured against the same baseline at the same SPRT params with seed `_00E`:

```
converged: elo=166.40 sigma=51.59 games=26 reason=sprt-h0
ci: elo=-110.48 [-207.37, -28.06] pairs=13
```

100 games requested, terminated at 26 with verdict=H0. The 300-game continuation (seed `_00F`) tracked to **~−44 Elo at 222 games** before being killed for the v2 refactor.

The v1→v2 retune (collapsing the per-stage state machine into a single `Vec<Move>` sorted once by `negamax_move_order_score`) restored per-node cost parity with M5.G and eliminated the regression. ADR-0030 §10 documents the diagnosis (3× per-node allocation pressure invisible to bench but visible under sustained game-load).
