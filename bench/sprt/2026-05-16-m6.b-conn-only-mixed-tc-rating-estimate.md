## Interpretation

The point estimate (~2648) sits ~30 below M6.A's ~2678 — but the two are
**independent 200-game HEAD-vs-Stockfish estimates**, not a head-to-head, and
their CIs overlap heavily (M6.B [2601, 2695] vs M6.A [2639, 2718]). A ~30-pt
point-estimate move between two such estimates is well inside the ±45–50
single-run noise band and is **not statistically distinguishable from flat**.
It is **not** evidence of a regression vs M6.A — the authoritative *relative*
measure is the direct head-to-head SPRT (+45.42 [+19.68, +71.67], CI excludes
0 with margin).

This is the documented, expected behaviour: `UCI_LimitStrength` is a
non-linear, noisy scale at 2200–2700 and Stockfish's internal node/skill
reduction does not track mixed-TC wallclock 1:1, so chained
clawfish-vs-clawfish logistic-Elo gains compress heavily and
non-monotonically into UCI_Elo space. M6.A's own calibration exhibited the
same pattern (a clearly-positive +250 internal SPRT compressing to a
+36.62 [−2.43, +76.61] UCI estimate whose CI lower bound *grazed* the
2641 anchor — directionally positive, not UCI-decisive). M6.B's smaller
internal delta (+45 vs M6.A) landing as a flat-within-noise UCI estimate is
the consistent continuation, not a contradiction.

**Curve datapoint:** M5.D-v2 ~2641 → M5.E ~2622 → M5.F ~2636 → M5.G ~2639 →
M6.A ~2678 [2639, 2718] → **M6.B CONN-only ~2648 [2601, 2695]** — flat
within noise across the M6.A→M6.B step on the UCI scale; the strength gain is
captured by the head-to-head SPRT chain, not the compressed UCI estimate.
