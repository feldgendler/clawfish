# M5.H2 — Staged movegen (lazy per-stage sorting)

**Plan unit.** `M5.H2`. With the H1 architectural wrapper in place, H2 swaps the eager single-`sort_by_cached_key` for a five-stage state machine that **lazy-sorts captures and quiets only when the consumer first enters the stage**. The stage enum (`Tt → Captures → Killer0 → Killer1 → Quiets → Done`) is resurrected from H1-v1 with two surviving Vecs (captures + quiets) instead of v1's three (`all` + captures + quiets — `all` is consumed by partition and dropped). Per-stage sort firings are gated by `bool` flags so a beta cutoff at an earlier stage skips the later sort entirely. The history-score timing benefit (research §15.6) is the literature Elo signal.

**Predecessor.** `baseline/m5h1-stager-refactor` (M5.H1 v2 thin-wrapper landing commit, tagged 2026-05-10).

**Research.** [`docs/research/m5-staged-movegen.md`](../research/m5-staged-movegen.md). Headlines: §7 lazy quiet generation skips quiet sort on early cutoff; §8 Elo signal +5–15 for an engine of clawfish's calibre; §15.6 history-score timing is the load-bearing literature signal that motivates lazy-after-search rather than lazy-before-search; §16 per-node cost characteristics; §19 synthesis.

**Expected size.** ~400–600 LOC including tests.

**Expected Elo.** **+5 to +15 Elo** per literature (research §8). SPRT-gated. Lower-end estimate (closer to +5) is realistic given clawfish already has strong move ordering (TT + killers + history + LMR + SE) — the marginal contribution of fresher history-scores at quiet sort time is modest. The cost side (per-node sort-firing tracking; one extra Vec allocation vs v2's one Vec) must be empirically dominated by the savings from skipped quiet sorts at cut-off nodes; this is the SPRT's job to measure, not bench's.

## §1. Scope and non-goals

**In scope.**
- Resurrect the `Stage` enum (`Tt`, `Captures`, `Killer0`, `Killer1`, `Quiets`, `Done`) and per-stage state in `MoveStager` — same shape as H1-v1, with `bool` flags `captures_sorted` / `quiets_sorted` gating the lazy per-stage `sort_by_cached_key` calls.
- Eager full generation at construction (`generate_moves` once, identical to v2 / M5.G) — generation is NOT being made lazy at H2; only the per-stage SORTS are lazy. Lazy generation is reserved for a future M5.H3.
- Eager partition into `captures: Vec<Move>` and `quiets: Vec<Move>` at construction. The intermediate `all` Vec is consumed by `partition_captures_quiets` (move semantics) and dropped. **H2 allocates 3 Vecs per stager construction (`all` + `captures` + `quiets`) — same per-node alloc count as v1**; the difference vs v1 is that `all` is dropped at partition return rather than retained. Live-memory footprint during the stager's lifetime: **2 long-lived Vecs** (captures + quiets), vs v1's 3 (which kept `all` alive alongside) and v2's 1 (single-Vec wrapper). The cost-savings claim relative to v1 is the lazy-sort skipping at cutoff nodes, NOT a reduction in alloc count — see §3.3 for the honest accounting.
- Eager TT extraction (`extract_move_by_bits`) and eager killer extraction (`extract_move_by_eq`) into `tt: Option<Move>`, `k0: Option<Move>`, `k1: Option<Move>` — identical logic to H1-v1.
- **Lazy MVV-LVA sort** of `captures` on first transition into `Stage::Captures` inside `next()`. Gated by `self.captures_sorted: bool`.
- **Lazy history sort** of `quiets` on first transition into `Stage::Quiets` inside `next()`. Gated by `self.quiets_sorted: bool`. **The lazy quiet sort is the literature Elo signal.**
- `MoveStager` carries no lifetime parameter and no `&HistoryTable` borrow. The stager stores `pos: Position` (Copy snapshot). The `&HistoryTable` reference is passed to `next()` as a per-call argument (`fn next(&mut self, history: &HistoryTable) -> Option<Move>`), so the borrow is held only for the body of `next()` (specifically only across the lazy `history_sort_in_place` call). After `next()` returns the borrow is released, leaving `&mut self.search_child(...)` recursion and in-loop `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff(&mut self.history_table, ...)` (on the cutoff arm) sound by construction. Approach 10D: per-call `&HistoryTable` parameter, structurally simpler than the rejected 10C lifetime-on-stager (which would have conflicted with `&mut self` in the loop body — see §3.2).
- Promote the v1 helpers (`extract_move_by_bits`, `extract_move_by_eq`, `partition_captures_quiets`, `mvv_lva_sort_in_place`, `history_sort_in_place`) from `#[cfg(test)]` back to production code paths. Their H1-H1..H10 mutation-discrimination tests carry over verbatim.
- New H2-* test surface targeting the lazy-sort firings counters (`#[cfg(test)] captures_sorts: u32` / `quiets_sorts: u32`).
- Bench refresh — no parity claim against `1147614 nodes`. Lazy-sort-after-search changes history-score timing → changes node counts → bench WILL drift. Plan does NOT pin a specific new value (the SPRT, not bench parity, is the strength gate).
- E51 depth-4 pin: also expected to drift; record new value at landing.
- Extended perft sweep per the standing movegen-adjacent rule.
- Mixed-TC SPRT vs `baseline/m5h1-stager-refactor` (seed `0xC1ABF15AE10DD011`).
- ADR-0030 §11 update (NOT a new ADR — H2 is a refinement of the H1 architecture, not a separate decision). Outline at §2.1.

**Out of scope (deferred to a future M5.H3 or later).**
- **Lazy generation** of captures and quiets. H2 still calls `generate_moves` once at construction. Lazy generation requires typed pseudo-legality validators for TT and killers (research §4–§5) plus a captures-only / quiets-only generator decomposition that today's `generate_moves` does not support. Future M5.H3 candidate.
- **Typed TT-move and killer validators.** H2 keeps the H1 membership-scan approach (TT extracted by `extract_move_by_bits`, killers by `extract_move_by_eq` over the legal-move list). Typed validators are required only when the legal list is not generated up-front.
- **SEE split** of captures into good (≥0) and bad (<0). MVV-LVA only.
- **Countermove stage.** M6+ candidate per CPW Countermove Heuristic.
- **Selection-sort within stages.** Both stages still use `sort_by_cached_key`.
- **Qsearch stager.** Out of scope per research §13.3.

### §2.1 ADR-0030 §11 outline ("M5.H2 lazy-sort retune")

ADR-0030 already documents H1's two-version arc (v1 stage-machine cost regression, v2 thin-wrapper retune). H2 adds §11 as a parallel structural section to §10:

- **§11.1 Symptom-and-diagnosis.** v2 thin-wrapper's single `negamax_move_order_score` sort is correct but does not capture the lazy-after-search history-timing literature signal — every node pays for sorting moves it never iterates after a beta cutoff.
- **§11.2 Decision.** Resurrect H1-v1's `Stage` enum + per-stage Vecs. Add `bool` flags `captures_sorted` / `quiets_sorted` gating per-stage `sort_by_cached_key`. Captures and quiets are partitioned eagerly at construction (1× `generate_moves`, 1× `partition_captures_quiets`, dropping the intermediate `all` — net 2 long-lived Vecs); the SORT is what's lazy.
- **§11.3 Borrow discipline.** `MoveStager` carries no `&Position` and no `&HistoryTable` field. Both references are passed as per-call arguments to `next(&mut self, pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable)`, so the borrows are held only across the lazy sort calls inside `next()`'s body. Between `next()` calls — i.e., in the move-loop body where `self.search_child(&mut self, ...)` recurses and where `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff(&mut self.history_table, ...)` runs on the cutoff path — the stager holds no borrows. `&mut self` is therefore free to flow into both recursion and history mutation without conflict. (The earlier `MoveStager<'a>` design was rejected: a stager-held `&'a HistoryTable` would conflict with every `&mut self` call in the loop body, including `self.search_child` recursion at non-cutoff iterations and `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff` at the in-loop cutoff path. The per-call argument form is the structurally simpler fix; symmetric application to `&Position` saves a ~210-byte Copy per stager construction.)
- **§11.4 Allocation accounting.** Per-node Vec allocations are **3** (the same as v1: `all`, `captures`, `quiets`); the difference vs v1 is that `all` is consumed at partition return rather than retained. Live memory during the stager's lifetime is 2 Vecs vs v1's 3. **The structural mitigation against v1's NPS regression is the lazy-sort savings at cutoff nodes (0–2 sort calls per node depending on cutoff position vs v1's always-2), NOT a reduction in per-node allocation count.** §12's sustained-load NPS gate is the empirical pre-SPRT check.
- **§11.5 Bench drift.** Lazy quiet sort runs after captures and killers have been searched, so history-score updates from those searches affect quiet ordering. This changes node counts. Bench at landing will NOT equal `1147614`. The new value is recorded at landing with no parity claim.
- **§11.6 SPRT signal.** Mixed-TC SPRT vs `baseline/m5h1-stager-refactor` is the strength gate. Outcomes per plan §13.
- **§11.7 Helpers reinstated.** The five v1 helpers (`extract_move_by_bits`, `extract_move_by_eq`, `partition_captures_quiets`, `mvv_lva_sort_in_place`, `history_sort_in_place`) become production code at H2; H1-H1..H10 tests carry over verbatim. `negamax_move_order_score` and the killer/capture score constants become `#[cfg(test)]`-only (the score-tier discipline survives only as a test-only invariant; H2's stager enforces the hierarchy structurally via stage separation).

## §3. The `MoveStager` type and API

```rust
/// M5.H2 — stage-aware iterator over the negamax move sequence with lazy
/// per-stage sorting.
///
/// **Yield order** (unchanged from H1):
///   1. TT move (if `tt_move \!= 0` and bits found in legal list).
///   2. Captures (and queen promos) sorted MVV-LVA descending — **sorted lazily
///      on first `Stage::Captures` entry**.
///   3. Killer slot 0 (post-validation: present in legal-quiets, distinct from TT).
///   4. Killer slot 1 (post-validation: present, distinct from TT and killer 0).
///   5. Remaining quiets sorted by history score descending — **sorted lazily
///      on first `Stage::Quiets` entry**, reading fresh history scores at
///      sort time (research §15.6 — the literature Elo signal).
///
/// **Generation discipline.** All moves are still generated by a single
/// `generate_moves` call at construction (eager generation; future M5.H3
/// may flip this to lazy per-stage generation with typed validators).
/// **Sorts** are lazy: the `mvv_lva_sort_in_place` and `history_sort_in_place`
/// calls fire only when the consumer's `next()` first transitions into the
/// respective stage. A beta cutoff at TT or captures means the quiet sort
/// never fires.
///
/// **Borrow discipline.** `MoveStager` carries no `&HistoryTable` borrow.
/// The `&HistoryTable` reference is passed to `next()` as a per-call
/// argument; the lazy quiet sort uses it only for the duration of the
/// `history_sort_in_place` call. The borrow is released when `next()`
/// returns, leaving `&mut self.search_child(...)` recursion and
/// `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff(&mut self.history_table, ...)` (in-loop
/// on cutoff) sound by construction. Contract documented at §3.2.
///
/// **Allocations.** Two long-lived Vecs: `captures` and `quiets`. The
/// intermediate `all: Vec<Move>` produced by `generate_moves` is consumed by
/// `partition_captures_quiets` (move semantics) and dropped — net 2 Vecs at
/// any given time during the stager's lifetime. (See §3.3 for the v1
/// regression hazard this allocation count is designed to dodge.)
pub(crate) struct MoveStager {
    // No `pos: Position` field — the &Position reference is passed to next()
    // as a per-call argument (symmetric with the &HistoryTable parameter).
    // The move loop's `pos: &Position` parameter outlives every `next()`
    // call, and `next()`'s temporary borrow composes harmlessly with the
    // `&Position` borrows elsewhere in the loop body. Saves ~210 bytes per
    // stager construction (Position is ~210 bytes by inspection of
    // `src/position.rs:110`). See §3.2 for the borrow discipline.

    // No `history: &HistoryTable` field — same per-call rationale.

    /// TT move, if extracted from the (filtered) legal list at construction.
    tt: Option<Move>,
    /// Captures + promotions, MVV-LVA-desc sorted **lazily**. Pre-sort
    /// state: movegen-emit order with TT excluded (if TT was a capture).
    /// Post-`captures_sorted` flag set: MVV-LVA-desc.
    captures: Vec<Move>,
    /// Killer slot 0 — extracted from quiets at construction (full Move eq;
    /// dedup against TT). `None` iff slot was 0/invalid/duplicate.
    k0: Option<Move>,
    /// Killer slot 1 — same as k0 with additional dedup against k0.
    k1: Option<Move>,
    /// Remaining quiets — history-desc sorted **lazily** on first
    /// `Stage::Quiets` entry. Pre-sort state: movegen-emit order with TT
    /// (if quiet), k0, k1 excluded.
    quiets: Vec<Move>,

    /// Stage state machine cursor.
    stage: Stage,
    /// Lazy-sort flags. Set on first transition into the corresponding
    /// stage; ensure the per-stage `sort_by_cached_key` runs at most once
    /// per stager. Also exposed via `_for_test` accessors for the H2-S* /
    /// H2-I* test surface (see §8).
    captures_sorted: bool,
    quiets_sorted: bool,

    /// Index of the next move to yield from `captures` (post-sort). Reset
    /// to 0 on first `Stage::Captures` entry; advanced by `next()` until
    /// the slice is exhausted, at which point `Stage::Killer0` is entered.
    cap_idx: usize,
    /// Same as `cap_idx` but for `quiets`.
    quiet_idx: usize,

    /// Pre-iteration total. Set at construction; never decremented by
    /// `next()` (temporal-contract pin: H1-S17 carries over).
    total_len: usize,

    /// Test-only counters for the H2 lazy-sort instrumentation. Incremented
    /// by `next()` on the lazy-sort firing edge.
    #[cfg(test)]
    captures_sorts: u32,
    #[cfg(test)]
    quiets_sorts: u32,
}

#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Debug)]
enum Stage {
    Tt,
    Captures,
    Killer0,
    Killer1,
    Quiets,
    Done,
}

impl MoveStager {
    /// Eagerly generate, optionally apply searchmoves filter, partition
    /// into captures + quiets, and extract TT + killer slots. **Captures
    /// and quiets are NOT sorted at construction** — sorts are lazy per
    /// stage in `next()`. No history parameter: the lazy quiet sort
    /// receives `&HistoryTable` from `next()`'s per-call argument.
    pub(crate) fn new(
        pos: &Position,
        killer0: Move,
        killer1: Move,
        tt_move: u16,
        searchmoves_filter: Option<&[Move]>,
    ) -> Self;

    /// Advance one move. Returns `None` once `Stage::Done` is reached.
    /// On first transition into `Stage::Captures` or `Stage::Quiets`,
    /// triggers the corresponding lazy sort exactly once. The `pos` and
    /// `history` references are borrowed only for the duration of this call
    /// (in particular, only across the lazy `mvv_lva_sort_in_place` call
    /// inside `Stage::Captures` and the lazy `history_sort_in_place` call
    /// inside `Stage::Quiets`). After `next()` returns, no borrow is held
    /// by the stager — `&mut self.search_child(...)` recursion and in-loop
    /// `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff(&mut self.history_table, ...)` are
    /// both sound.
    pub(crate) fn next(&mut self, pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable) -> Option<Move>;

    /// Return what `next()` would yield without advancing.
    ///
    /// **Production-call invariant.** All M5.G/M5.H1 production callers
    /// of `peek()` (the SE block at `src/search.rs:1737-1782`) call it
    /// before any `next()` call (i.e., when `self.stage == Stage::Tt`).
    /// Peek at `Stage::Tt` reads `self.tt` directly — no lazy sort, no
    /// `&mut self`. This is the load-bearing invariant for keeping
    /// `peek()` `&self`-receiver and idempotent for the SE block's
    /// double-call. Pinned by H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage.
    ///
    /// **Defense-in-depth.** A `debug_assert\!(self.stage == Stage::Tt)`
    /// inside `peek()` (debug builds only) would catch a future regression
    /// where peek() is called at non-Tt stages — but introducing such an
    /// assertion would change `peek()`'s behavior. Instead, we encode the
    /// invariant in a unit test (H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage) plus
    /// a documented contract in the doc-comment. If a future consumer
    /// calls peek() at Stage::Captures or Stage::Quiets, the test surface
    /// must be updated to match the new contract.
    pub(crate) fn peek(&self) -> Option<Move>;

    /// Pre-iteration total. Same temporal contract as H1.
    #[allow(dead_code)]
    pub(crate) fn len(&self) -> usize;

    /// `true` iff `len() == 0`.
    pub(crate) fn is_empty(&self) -> bool;

    /// Test-only: materialise the full yield sequence by repeatedly calling
    /// `next(pos, history)` on `&mut self`. Forces both lazy sorts (captures
    /// + quiets). The `pos` and `history` references are forwarded to every
    /// internal `next()` call. Used by H2-P1 and the H2-S* yield-order tests.
    /// (Receiver is `&mut self` because `next()` is `&mut self`; tests that
    /// want a non-consuming form should clone the stager first.)
    #[cfg(test)]
    pub(crate) fn yield_sequence(&mut self, pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable) -> Vec<Move>;

    /// Test-only accessor for the lazy-sort firings counter on captures.
    #[cfg(test)]
    pub(crate) fn captures_sorts_for_test(&self) -> u32;

    /// Test-only accessor for the lazy-sort firings counter on quiets.
    #[cfg(test)]
    pub(crate) fn quiets_sorts_for_test(&self) -> u32;
}
```

### §3.1 The `total_len` temporal contract (carried over from H1)

`total_len` is `tt.is_some() + captures.len() + k0.is_some() + k1.is_some() + quiets.len()`, computed at construction. `len()` returns it without decrement. `is_empty()` is meaningful only before any `next()` call. **`MoveStager` does NOT impl `Iterator`** — same justification as H1's §3.1, AND additionally: `Iterator::next` has signature `fn next(&mut self) -> Option<Self::Item>` (no extra args), so the per-call `pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable` arguments on H2's `next()` make the `Iterator` trait *signaturally* unimplementable. Pleasant accidental property — reinforces the temporal contract that `len()` does not decrement.

### §3.2 The borrow discipline for `&Position` and `&HistoryTable` at `next()` calls

`MoveStager` carries no `&Position` and no `&HistoryTable` fields. Both references are supplied as per-call arguments to `next(&mut self, pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable)`. The borrow's soundness rests on these properties:

1. **No long-lived borrow.** Between `next()` calls — that is, in the move-loop body of `negamax` — the stager holds NO borrow of `pos` or `self.history_table`. The caller may freely take `&mut self` (and thereby `&mut self.history_table`) for any purpose: `self.search_child(...)` recursion at non-cutoff iterations, or `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff(&mut self.history_table, ...)` on the in-loop cutoff path at line 2033 of `src/search.rs`.
2. **Borrow scope = `next()` body.** Inside `next()`, the `pos: &Position` parameter is held only across the lazy `mvv_lva_sort_in_place(&mut self.captures, pos)` call (Stage::Captures, gated by `!self.captures_sorted && !self.captures.is_empty()`); the `history: &HistoryTable` parameter is held only across the lazy `history_sort_in_place(&mut self.quiets, pos, history)` call (Stage::Quiets, gated similarly). Both borrows are released when `next()` returns.
3. **Fresh-history semantics.** Because `next()` re-borrows `&self.history_table` at every call, the lazy quiet sort (when it fires) reads whatever `self.history_table` contains AT THAT MOMENT — including any updates written by `update_history_on_quiet_cutoff` at sibling cutoff paths in earlier `search_child` recursions. This is the literature Elo signal (research §15.6): history is sampled at sort time, not at construction time.
4. **No inner scope block needed.** An earlier H2-design draft wrapped the move loop in an inner `{ ... }` so a stager-held `&'a HistoryTable` borrow would drop before the post-loop history update. That design was structurally unsound: the history update is IN-LOOP (inside the cutoff `if alpha >= beta` arm at `src/search.rs:2033`), not post-loop, so an inner scope can't release the borrow in time. And recursive `self.search_child(...)` calls in the loop body would also conflict with a stager-held shared borrow of `self.history_table`. The per-call-argument form sidesteps both problems by holding no borrow between calls.
5. **Symmetric `&Position` per-call.** The earlier draft of this plan stored `pos: Position` as a Copy snapshot in the stager. That works (Position is Copy, no borrow involved), but adds ~210 bytes of struct-copy work per stager construction. Since the plan already pays the borrow-discipline cost of per-call `&HistoryTable`, it is symmetrically free for `&Position`: the move-loop's `pos: &Position` parameter outlives every `next()` call, and shared borrows of `pos` compose harmlessly with `pos.make_move(mv)` style usage inside `search_child` (which takes `pos: &mut Position` from a separate copy at the call boundary, not the parent's `&Position`).

Alternative (rejected): pass `&HistoryTable` once at construction via a stager lifetime parameter `MoveStager<'a>`. Rejected because every `&mut self` call in the move loop (recursion AND in-loop history mutation) would conflict with the stager's shared borrow.

### §3.3 Allocation accounting and the v1-regression hazard

**Honest cost picture.** H1-v1 made **3 `Vec<Move>` allocations per node** (`all`, `captures`, `quiets`) and ran **2 sort calls** (one per non-empty stage) per node. ADR-0030 §10 attributed v1's sustained-load NPS regression specifically to this per-node allocation churn and the second sort call — both invisible to the depth-7 bench snapshot but visible at depth 14 / 20-sequential-go.

**H2's per-node allocations are also 3:** `all` (from `ml.iter().collect()` at Step A), `captures` (inside `partition_captures_quiets` at Step D), and `quiets` (also inside `partition_captures_quiets`). The only difference vs v1 is that `all` is consumed by partition (move semantics) and dropped immediately, rather than retained alongside captures + quiets through the stager's lifetime. **Per-node alloc/dealloc count is the same as v1**; live-memory footprint is lower (2 Vecs alive at any point vs v1's 3).

**Where H2 is cheaper than v1, then.** Not on per-node allocation count — only on:
1. **Live memory** during the stager's lifetime: 2 Vecs vs v1's 3 (irrelevant for cache locality at typical Vec sizes — 30-element `Vec<Move>` ≈ 60 bytes — but documented for completeness).
2. **Lazy sort skipping at cutoff nodes**: when the TT or captures stage causes a beta cutoff before quiets are entered, the quiet sort is skipped entirely. v1 always ran 2 sorts (1 per non-empty stage); H2 runs 0–2 depending on cutoff position. **This is the load-bearing cost-savings claim.**

**Therefore:** the structural mitigation against the v1 regression is NOT "lower allocation count" — it is the **empirical lazy-sort savings** at cutoff nodes, plus the §12 verification-gate sustained-load NPS measurement (avg ratio ≥ 0.97 vs `baseline/m5h1-stager-refactor`) **before** committing the SPRT wallclock. If the savings don't dominate the (unchanged-from-v1) per-node alloc cost, the §12 gate catches it; if §12 passes but SPRT is still negative, that's the SPRT's job to detect. The §11 risk register tracks this honestly.

H2 is closer to v1 than to v2 on the allocation axis. The H2 cost claim is empirical, not structural.

## §4. Construction algorithm

In pseudo-Rust:

```rust
pub(crate) fn new(
    pos: &Position,
    killer0: Move,
    killer1: Move,
    tt_move: u16,
    searchmoves_filter: Option<&[Move]>,
) -> Self {
    use crate::movegen::generate_moves;

    // Step A. Eager full generation (unchanged from H1-v2).
    let mut ml = MoveList::new();
    generate_moves(pos, &mut ml);
    let mut all: Vec<Move> = ml.iter().collect();

    // Step B. Apply searchmoves filter (root-only; None at non-root).
    if let Some(filter) = searchmoves_filter {
        all.retain(|m| filter.contains(m));
    }

    // Step C. Extract TT move (membership lookup by bits; order-preserving).
    //   Same logic as H1-v1; `extract_move_by_bits` is now production code
    //   (un-cfg-tested per §1.1).
    let tt = extract_move_by_bits(&mut all, tt_move);

    // Step D. Partition into captures + quiets. `all` is moved in and
    //   consumed; dropped at the partition return boundary. After this
    //   line, the stager owns only `captures` and `quiets` — net 2 Vecs.
    let (mut captures, mut quiets) = partition_captures_quiets(all);

    // Step E. Extract killer slots from quiets.
    //   `bits() \!= 0` short-circuit guards against the Move::default sentinel.
    //   `killer1 \!= killer0` short-circuit dedups slot 1 against slot 0.
    let k0 = if killer0.bits() \!= 0 {
        extract_move_by_eq(&mut quiets, killer0)
    } else {
        None
    };
    let k1 = if killer1.bits() \!= 0 && killer1 \!= killer0 {
        extract_move_by_eq(&mut quiets, killer1)
    } else {
        None
    };

    // Step F. Compute total length BEFORE any sort. Captures and quiets
    //   are unsorted but their LENGTHS are final.
    let total_len = tt.is_some() as usize
        + captures.len()
        + k0.is_some() as usize
        + k1.is_some() as usize
        + quiets.len();

    // Step G. **NO SORT AT CONSTRUCTION.** captures_sorted = false /
    //   quiets_sorted = false. The lazy sorts fire in `next()` on first
    //   transition into the corresponding stage.

    Self {
        // No `pos` or `history` fields — both are passed per-call to next().
        tt,
        captures,
        k0,
        k1,
        quiets,
        stage: Stage::Tt,
        captures_sorted: false,
        quiets_sorted: false,
        cap_idx: 0,
        quiet_idx: 0,
        total_len,
        #[cfg(test)] captures_sorts: 0,
        #[cfg(test)] quiets_sorts: 0,
    }
}
```

### §4.1 The `next()` state machine

```rust
pub(crate) fn next(&mut self, pos: &Position, history: &HistoryTable) -> Option<Move> {
    loop {
        match self.stage {
            Stage::Tt => {
                self.stage = Stage::Captures;
                if let Some(mv) = self.tt {
                    return Some(mv);
                }
                // Fall through to Captures.
            }
            Stage::Captures => {
                // Skip the no-op sort on empty captures (the counter stays
                // at 0 in that case — matches the H2-S-lazy-position-no-
                // captures-stays-zero test name's intent).
                if \!self.captures_sorted && \!self.captures.is_empty() {
                    // **Lazy MVV-LVA sort — fires at most once per stager.**
                    // `pos` is the per-call argument — borrow held only
                    // across this sort call, released when next() returns.
                    mvv_lva_sort_in_place(&mut self.captures, pos);
                    self.captures_sorted = true;
                    #[cfg(test)] { self.captures_sorts += 1; }
                }
                if self.cap_idx < self.captures.len() {
                    let mv = self.captures[self.cap_idx];
                    self.cap_idx += 1;
                    return Some(mv);
                }
                self.stage = Stage::Killer0;
                // Fall through.
            }
            Stage::Killer0 => {
                self.stage = Stage::Killer1;
                if let Some(mv) = self.k0 {
                    return Some(mv);
                }
            }
            Stage::Killer1 => {
                self.stage = Stage::Quiets;
                if let Some(mv) = self.k1 {
                    return Some(mv);
                }
            }
            Stage::Quiets => {
                // Skip the no-op sort on empty quiets (counter stays at 0).
                if \!self.quiets_sorted && \!self.quiets.is_empty() {
                    // **Lazy history sort — fires at most once per stager.**
                    //   Reads fresh history scores at this point — captures
                    //   and killers may have updated history during their
                    //   recursive searches. Research §15.6 — the literature
                    //   Elo signal.
                    // `pos` and `history` are per-call arguments — borrows
                    // held only across this sort call, released on return.
                    history_sort_in_place(&mut self.quiets, pos, history);
                    self.quiets_sorted = true;
                    #[cfg(test)] { self.quiets_sorts += 1; }
                }
                if self.quiet_idx < self.quiets.len() {
                    let mv = self.quiets[self.quiet_idx];
                    self.quiet_idx += 1;
                    return Some(mv);
                }
                self.stage = Stage::Done;
            }
            Stage::Done => return None,
        }
    }
}
```

The `loop { match }` pattern with explicit fall-through on stage transitions is the same idiom as research §14.2 and H1-v1's `next()`.

### §4.2 The `peek()` invariant

```rust
pub(crate) fn peek(&self) -> Option<Move> {
    // Production callers (the M5.G SE block at src/search.rs:1737-1782)
    // call peek() ONLY when self.stage == Stage::Tt — i.e., before any
    // next() call. At Stage::Tt, peek reads self.tt without mutation.
    // Pinned by H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage (§8).
    //
    // Non-Tt stages PANIC rather than silently return a pre-sort move.
    // Returning `self.captures.get(self.cap_idx)` at Stage::Captures before
    // the lazy MVV-LVA sort has fired would yield a value that LOOKS legit
    // (it's a real legal move) but is **not** the head of MVV-LVA-desc order
    // — a silent-bad-data footgun. The `unreachable!()` panic fails loudly
    // and matches the project's discipline (workflow.md §"Final review loop"):
    // provably-unreachable branches must panic, not silently degrade.
    match self.stage {
        Stage::Tt => self.tt,
        Stage::Captures
        | Stage::Killer0
        | Stage::Killer1
        | Stage::Quiets
        | Stage::Done => unreachable!(
            "MoveStager::peek() is valid only at Stage::Tt; \
             see ADR-0030 §11 and H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage"
        ),
    }
}
```

The non-Tt arms `unreachable!()` rather than returning a pre-sort move. The discipline: a peek that returns `self.captures.get(self.cap_idx)` before the lazy MVV-LVA sort has fired would silently return a legitimate-looking move that is NOT the MVV-LVA-desc head — exactly the kind of silent-bad-data footgun the project's `unreachable!()` convention exists to prevent (workflow.md §"Final review loop"). Loud panic now → forced redesign at the time a future consumer needs non-Tt peek.

If a future consumer (e.g., a hypothetical M5.H3 producing a typed-validate flow) needs to call `peek()` at non-Tt stages, the redesign options are: (a) interior mutability via `Cell<bool>` for the sort flags so peek can fire the lazy sort under `&self`; (b) `peek_mut(&mut self) -> Option<Move>` named variant that fires lazy sorts; (c) eager-sort fallback removing the lazy bit entirely. The plan does not pre-commit to any of these — the panic forces an explicit choice when the need arises.

The H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage test pins the production invariant by NOT panicking — calling `peek()` at non-Tt stages from within the test would panic, so the test only ever calls peek with `stage == Stage::Tt`.

### §8.4 H2-P — Equivalence proptest (the H1-P1 redefinition)

H1-P1 asserted that the H1-v1/v2 stager's `yield_sequence` was byte-equivalent to today's `order_moves` output. At H2, this no longer holds in production: the lazy quiet sort reads `&HistoryTable` (passed per-call to `next()`) AT SORT TIME, but `order_moves` reads it at construction time. If the history table mutates between construction and the moment `next()` first enters Stage::Quiets, the orderings diverge.

The H2-P1 redefinition: equivalence holds when **the history table is held constant from construction to full iteration** (i.e., the same `&HistoryTable` is passed to every `next()` call AND that table's contents do not mutate during iteration). The proptest fixture freezes the history table by snapshot (one shared `&HistoryTable` reference threaded through every `next()` call; never mutated during the test) and asserts byte-equivalence:

## §10. Mutation-coverage strategy

Per `cargo mutants --in-diff`. Mutant categories:

**Pure-helper mutants** — H1-H1..H10 carry over verbatim. The five helpers are now production code, but their bodies are unchanged.

**Lazy-sort flag mutants:**
- `if \!self.captures_sorted` → `if self.captures_sorted`: inverts the flag check. Mutant fires the captures sort REPEATEDLY (every `next()` call at Stage::Captures triggers `mvv_lva_sort_in_place`). H2-S-lazy-captures-fires-on-first-capture-yield catches via `captures_sorts == 1` after multiple yields.
- `self.captures_sorted = true` → `self.captures_sorted = false`: never sets the flag; sort runs every iteration. Same catch surface.
- Same pattern for `self.quiets_sorted`.

**Stage-transition mutants:**
- `self.stage = Stage::Captures` → `self.stage = Stage::Killer0`: skips the captures stage entirely. Captures never yielded. H2-S* yield-order tests + carryover H1-P1 catch.
- Stage enum reordering / pass-through mutations: H1-P1 carryover (now H2-P1) is the broad backstop.

**Lazy-sort firing instrumentation mutants:**
- `self.captures_sorts += 1` → `self.captures_sorts -= 1` (negation): catches via H2-S-lazy-captures-fires-on-first-capture-yield asserting `== 1`.
- `self.captures_sorts += 1` → no-op: catches via the same test.

**`next()` history-parameter mutants:**
- `history_sort_in_place(&mut self.quiets, pos, history)` → `history_sort_in_place(&mut self.quiets, pos, &HistoryTable::default())`: passes a fresh empty history instead of the per-call argument. Caught by H2-S-next-with-fresh-history-uses-fresh-scores: the test yields the wrong order if the per-call history-arg is ignored.
- `next(&mut self, history: &HistoryTable)` body where `history` is shadowed or unused: same catch surface — the literature-signal test detects any case where `history` is not the one used at sort time.

**Expected survivors** (triaged at landing):
- `captures_sorts += 1` ≡ `captures_sorts |= 1`: equivalent because (a) the counter starts at 0 (initialized in the `Self { ... }` literal at construction), (b) the `!self.captures_sorted` flag ensures the increment fires at most once per stager lifetime, and (c) `0 + 1 == 0 | 1 == 1`. Mutating `+=` to `|=` is observationally indistinguishable under the H2-S test surface. Document via `exclude_re`.
- `Vec::with_capacity(all.len())` capacity arithmetic in `partition_captures_quiets`: behaviorally invisible (allocator-policy difference only); document via `exclude_re`.

Net mutation coverage at H2: H1-H/S helper coverage carries over; H2 adds instrumentation coverage for the new lazy-sort flag surface; the proptest H2-P1 is the broad equivalence backstop.

## §13. SPRT gate criteria

Per the M5.F / M5.G precedent (plan §11 outcomes 1/2/3/4):

- **Outcome 1 — H1 accepted (LLR crosses upper bound).** Δ Elo positive with strong signal. **Land.**
- **Outcome 2 — `verdict=continue` with mean ≥ 5 Elo and CI lower > -10.** Small-but-not-regression — the M5.F / M5.G v2 precedent. **Land** with diagnostic-suite confirmation (WAC + STS within ±2-position-band wallclock noise).
- **Outcome 3 — `verdict=continue` with mean positive but CI lower < -10.** **Default: don't land.** H2 is a strength-claim unit (literature +5–15 Elo); the M5.E "correctness-only" precedent does not apply. A discretionary land is permitted only if (a) diagnostic suites (WAC + STS) show CLEAR positive signal beyond ±wallclock-noise (not just "flat"), AND (b) per-TC breakdown is non-pathological (no decisive negative bucket). Otherwise descope to outcome 4.
- **Outcome 4 — `verdict=H0` (LLR crosses lower bound) OR mean negative OR outcome 3 default.** **Don't land.** Investigate. If the §12 sustained-load gate had passed but SPRT is still negative, the lazy-sort savings did not dominate the per-node alloc cost in real games (predicted in §3.3 as the load-bearing empirical claim). Descope: H2 is documented in ADR-0030 §11 as "lazy-sort attempt rejected; M5.H proceeds directly to H3 (typed lazy generation) without the lazy-sort intermediate." Bench, mutants, and tests for the H2 stager design are preserved as test-only since they re-pin the H1 v2 thin-wrapper's correctness.

**Wallclock budget for SPRT: ~2-3 hours.** Mixed-TC, 400-game cap is the M5.F-G precedent for outcome-2-likely tests.

## §15. Rollout for any future M5.H3 (informational)

H3 would change `MoveStager::new` to defer `generate_moves` entirely:
- TT stage: typed `is_pseudo_legal` validator (no list scan).
- Captures stage: `generate_captures(pos, &mut self.captures)` on first entry.
- Killer stages: typed validator.
- Quiets stage: `generate_quiets(pos, &mut self.quiets)` on first entry.

H3 requires a captures-only / quiets-only generator decomposition that today's `generate_moves` does not support — likely a ~few-day implementation effort in `src/movegen/`. H3's Elo expectation is +5–15 over H2 (the cost-side of generation is the savings; H2 already captures the history-timing benefit). H3's plan will be a separate document.

The H2-S-peek-only-fires-at-tt-stage invariant is also load-bearing for H3 (peek at non-Tt stages becomes "trigger lazy generation," which requires `&mut self`). H3 will need to either (a) require `&mut self` on `peek()` (breaking the SE block's current double-call pattern, requiring `peek()` to memoise its result in a `Cell<Option<Move>>`), or (b) preserve the "peek only at Tt" invariant the SE block already obeys.
