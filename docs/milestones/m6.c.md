# M6.C — Passed pawns (retrospective)

Third M6 sub-phase. Lands the **passed-pawn evaluation infrastructure**
(per-passer rank bonus + EG three-state path discriminator + EG king-tropism,
all reading M6.B's cached `passed[2]`, computed live in `evaluate_core`)
**shipped score-neutral** — every passed-pawn weight zeroed in `eval::data`,
the entire weight set deferred to the M6.F joint-Texel reshape. The
literature-default weights SPRT'd through a three-config screen ladder vs
`M6.B` and were proven to have a **scale-invariant structural mismatch** with
this engine: no global scalar makes them non-negative across the TC profile.
This is the M6.B `(ISO+CONN)/2`-plateau lesson reproduced for passed pawns.
M6.C lands behaviorally inert vs `M6.B` (bench `1213649` byte-for-byte).

## What landed

- **`passed_pawn_term_white(pos, &passed) -> (i32, i32)`** in
  `src/eval/pawns.rs` — white-perspective, three additive per-passer
  sub-terms: rank bonus (`PASSED_MG/EG[rel]`); EG three-state path
  discriminator (`±PASSED_FREE_EG_DELTA[rel]`: empty-of-all-pieces → +Δ,
  enemy piece on the front-span → −Δ, friendly-only → 0); EG king-tropism

  enemy-king-near penalty, clamped at `PASSED_KDIST_CAP=5`). Computed **live**
  in `evaluate_core` reading M6.B's cached `pe.passed` — king-distance/path
  are not pawn-only so the term is never cached (ADR-0032 §3).
- **Shipped config: all weights zeroed.** `PASSED_MG = PASSED_EG =
  PASSED_FREE_EG_DELTA = [0; 8]`, `PASSED_KDIST_OWN_PER_STEP =
  PASSED_KDIST_ENEMY_PER_STEP = 0` in `src/eval/data.rs`;
  `PASSED_KDIST_CAP = 5` kept as the named structural clamp. The term math
  stays live at zero weight (M6.F-ready) — the M6.B `PAWN_STRUCTURE_IN_EVAL`

  enters the blend numerator only — never `static_eval_white()` (the

  pinned by `mop_up_addend_excludes_passed_pawns_vs_m6b`).
- **No new ADR** (roadmap M6.C row commits the path-blocked semantic);
  ADR-0032 gains a §8 M6.C addendum. No `PawnEval`/`PawnHashTable`/
  pawn-Zobrist/make-unmake/movegen change.

## The screen ladder (the cheap-Elo attempt that did not pay out)

The plan shipped all three sub-terms at literature defaults (MadChess
co-calibrated rank+path; placeholder king-distance) and SPRT'd vs `M6.B` at
the canonical mixed-TC, elo0=0/elo1=5. The pre-committed §11 contingency
ladder ran unattended:

| config | Δ Elo vs `M6.B` | per-TC story |
|---|---|---|
| all-three (literature) | **−21.74** [−49.60, +5.84] | 60+0.6 **W15-L57** (KDIST collapse) |
| {RANK+PATH} (KDIST off) | +4.34 [−22.33, +31.07] | 10+0.1 **W30-L59** (fast-TC over-mag) |
| {RANK+PATH}/2 (co-scale) | −0.87 [−23.68, +21.94] | 20+0.2 **W15-L42** (failure migrated) |

- **R2 vindicated — KDIST is the slow-TC killer.** Removing the
  independently-sourced, not-co-calibrated, rank-scaled king-distance term
  (the ADR-0032 §7 *de-modulated-fragment + standalone-coefficient* pastiche
  pattern) took 60+0.6 from −42 to +18: a +60 swing.
- **R1 vindicated — RANK+PATH is fast-TC over-magnitude.** The MadChess rank
  table was tuned against a *different* EG pawn PST; stacked on our PeSTO EG
  pawn PST it reaches ~+300–350 EG for a rank-6 passer (research §4.2),
  over-valuing passers at shallow depth.
- **The `{RANK+PATH}/2` probe is decisive.** A uniform downscale does not
  rescue to positive — it **migrates the over-magnitude failure across TC
  buckets** (10+0.1 fixed +24, 20+0.2 now −27). This is the M6.B
  `(ISO+CONN)/2` plateau reproduced: a scale-invariant structural residual no
  global multiplier fixes. Per ADR-0032 §7, *only a joint reshape can*.

No positive interaction-immune subset exists (contrast M6.B's clean CONN-only
+103 H1). `{RANK}` / `{RANK+KDIST}` were not screened — KDIST is confirmed
slow-TC-toxic so any KDIST-bearing subset inherits the −42 collapse;
`{RANK}`-alone is *more* PeSTO-EG-PST-overlapping than the already-failed
`{RANK+PATH}`; the `{RANK+PATH}/2` plateau is the decisive scale-invariant
verdict across the family. This is the M6.B *ship-known-positive-or-defer,
don't exhaustively screen a structurally-confirmed-bad family* precedent ⇒
the **entire** passed-pawn weight set defers to M6.F.

## Score-neutral landing

The shipped build zeroes every passed weight ⇒ `passed_pawn_term_white`
returns `(0,0)` for all positions ⇒ `evaluate` is byte-identical to `M6.B`.
**Landing gate (M6.B precedent verbatim):** zeroed-weights `bench` ==
predecessor byte-for-byte ⇒ the infrastructure is *provably behaviorally
inert* ⇒ regression structurally impossible ⇒ M6.C lands **without a
confirmation SPRT**. Verified: `bench: 1213649 nodes` / depth-4 `90591`,
deterministic ×2, byte-identical to `M6.B`.

## Collateral: 4 search-test fixtures + the illegal-fixture discovery

M6.C's live-term eval (during the screen, before the score-neutral pivot)
reshuffled search ordering and broke 4 pre-existing `src/search.rs` tests
calibrated against the M6.B eval. The shipped score-neutral build's eval ==
`M6.B`, so 3 were **reverted byte-identical to `git show M6.B:src/search.rs`**
(`negamax_horizon_calls_qsearch_not_evaluate`,
`qsearch_under_promo_finds_winning_rook_promo_when_queen_stalemates`,
`id_loop_aborts_during_second_aspiration_try_breaks_outer_iter_cleanly`).

The 4th, `negamax_skips_nmp_when_in_check`, exposed a **pre-existing
test-fixture defect**: both its M6.B-era fixtures (`7k/8/8/8/3Q4/…`) are
**FIDE-illegal** — White Qd4's open d4→h8 diagonal puts the black king in
check with White to move; the fixture-validation only checked the
side-to-move's check, never the opponent's. Latent because old move-ordering
never explored the king-capture branch; M6.C's screen reorder surfaced
`generate_moves` emitting `Qd4xh8`, tripping `debug_assert! src/mov.rs:589`.
**Not an engine bug** — legal-direct movegen (ADR-0007) makes
opponent-in-check-while-to-move unreachable in real play. **Kept** in its
M6.C legal-fixture form (Qb3 fixtures + a new `opponent_in_check` legality
assertion) as an eval-independent correctness improvement: reverting would
knowingly re-ship an illegal fixture (M5.E correctness-over-Elo precedent).
Under score-neutral eval: `nodes_in=38 > nodes_no=2`, discriminator holds.

## Lessons learned

- **Co-calibrated-elsewhere ≠ transfers here.** The MadChess rank+path pair
  is a single Texel run, yet stacked on *our* PeSTO EG pawn PST it
  over-magnitudes at fast TC; the placeholder king-distance over-scales at
  slow TC. "Literature default from a coherent source" is still not safe to
  ship raw — the M6.B lesson generalizes beyond same-axis double-counts to
  *any* cross-engine weight transfer onto a different eval baseline.
- **A TC-localized collapse is the over-scaled-EG fingerprint.** all-three's
  failure was invisible at 10+0.1 and catastrophic only at 60+0.6 — the
  inverse of M6.B's CONN-only (fast-strong, slow-negative). A single-TC
  screen would have been blind to it; the plan's pre-committed
  "screen-must-run-at-the-canonical-mixed-TC-when-the-profile-is-TC-dependent"
  guard (§11) earned its keep. **Always read `summary-by-tc` before
  concluding from the aggregate.**
- **The co-scale probe distinguishes over-magnitude from wrong-shape — and
  here it said wrong-shape.** `{RANK+PATH}/2` fixed 10+0.1 but broke 20+0.2:
  a uniform multiplier cannot make a wrong-shaped term non-negative across
  the TC profile, it just slides the failure. This is the M6.B `(ISO+CONN)/2`
  finding reproduced; it converts M6.F's brief from "rescale" to "reshape"
  (re-derive the rank table against our EG PST; re-shape — not just rescale —
  king-distance) and is the reason no global-scalar M6.C subset shipped.
- **An eval-magnitude screen is a cheap illegal-fixture detector.** M6.C's
  reorder surfaced a latent FIDE-illegal test fixture (opponent-in-check-
  while-to-move) the M6.B-era validation missed by only checking the
  side-to-move. The generalizable fix — assert the *opponent* is also not in
  check — is now in `negamax_skips_nmp_when_in_check`; the same gap likely
  exists in other hand-built search fixtures (a tooling-backlog candidate).
- **Score-neutral landing keeps the milestone moving.** Like M6.B, M6.C lands
  the infrastructure (term math, three-state path, king-distance formula,
  `passed[]` consumption, the bug-taxonomy test surface) inert; M6.D/M6.E
  build on it; M6.F joint-Texel calibrates the passed weights jointly with
  the M6.B-deferred ISO/DBL/BWD + CONN rescale. No Elo was *lost* (inert vs
  `M6.B`); the cost was ~40 h of screen SPRT that produced a precise,
  documented M6.F brief instead of a blind +X.
