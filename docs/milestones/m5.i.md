# M5.I — Third aspiration tier (DEFERRED, 2026-05-11)

**Outcome**: Elo-neutral at full N=400; reverted per plan §11 outcome 2. Production HEAD stays at `M5.H1`.

**Date**: 2026-05-11.

## Summary

M5.I attempted the deferred-from-M4.D third aspiration tier: insert an intermediate widening step between M4.D's `±50 cp` first try and the existing asymmetric full-window fallback. The implementation followed the literature consensus (Crafty's `[g − 1/4, g + 1]` schedule, lucasart's proved-bound-preservation rule) with a Crafty-proportional `±150 cp` intermediate half-width.

Mixed-TC SPRT vs `M5.H1` (seed `0xC1ABF15AE10DD011`, 4-bucket uniform TC mix): **Δ Elo +1.74 [−22.00, +25.49]** at 400 games / 200 pairs, verdict=continue, LLR=−0.22. All four per-TC buckets converged to ~50% ± noise. No measurable per-TC signal. Plan §11 outcome 2 → no-change retrospective + revert.

## What worked

- **Plan and review-loop discipline**. Plan-review converged in 3 passes; test-suite review in 2 passes; final review in 1 pass. Helper signature redesigned mid-plan-review (the original `widen_intermediate(returned, prev, prev_alpha, prev_beta)` was flagged as redundant/coupling-prone and collapsed to `(returned, prev_alpha, prev_beta)`-only).
- **Implementation correctness**. Zero mutation-survivors (22/22 caught) on the diff. Helper-side semantics (proved-bound preservation, inverted-window skip subsuming mate) pinned cleanly by 10 unit + 5 integration tests.
- **Empirical fixture coverage**. The plan acknowledged AS34c (tier-2-fires-then-tier-3-fires) as fixture-uncertain; impl-step 2 empirical observation confirmed `fail_high_fixture` triggers exactly this case, closing the cap-pin gap without ad-hoc fixture construction.
- **Bench regression detection by structural test**. Bench at depth 7 startpos changed by −0.45% (1142435 vs M5.H1's 1147614). The plan §12 predicted "unchanged ± 0.1%"; the structural cause was identifiable (tier 2 fires at some bench positions at depth ≥ 6, saving nodes). Not a regression, but worth noting that AS11's stable-fixture characterization at depths 4-5 didn't generalize to bench's depth 7.

## What didn't work

- **The mechanism itself produced no measurable Elo.** The literature's predicted +10-20 Elo from a third tier (Crafty / Meesha schedules) is not realized at clawfish's current strength/TC profile. Plausible reasons:
  - At typical TCs (mixed 10+0.1 through 60+0.6), clawfish reaches depth 8-12. Score-continuity at those depths isn't strong enough to make tier-2's narrow re-search frequently succeed; nor is it weak enough to produce the high-volume small failures the intermediate tier targets.
  - The inverted-window skip fires frequently at deeper iterations (large fail-high magnitudes from horizon-effect surprises), so tier 2 is bypassed and the loop reverts to M4.D's two-tier behavior — no harm, but also no benefit.
  - The bimodal pattern predicted by the M5.G/M5.H2 precedent (fast-TC win / slow-TC loss, or vice versa) was **not** observed at full N. The 168-game checkpoint showed apparent bimodality (10+0.1 +41, 20+0.2 −71) that dissolved by 400 games. The early signal was Bernoulli noise at N≈34 per bucket.

- **Mid-SPRT per-TC-iteration directive proved misleading.** A user-directed v2 design ("salvage fast-TC benefit without losing slow-TC") was scoped during the 168-game checkpoint when the early data looked clearly bimodal. By 400 games the premise was falsified: no fast-TC benefit existed to salvage. The lesson: per-TC reads at <200 games are too noisy to anchor follow-up tuning decisions. The M5.G v1 → v2 retune precedent worked because v1's bimodal signal at 400 games was magnitude-strong and direction-consistent; M5.I had no such surviving signal.

## Lessons (for future feature attempts)

1. **Wait for full SPRT completion before designing follow-up variants.** Per-TC reads at <200 games (N≈34-50 per bucket) have ±10-12% CIs — easily encompassing the +/− 40-Elo range that looks dramatic in real-time. Mid-SPRT pattern-fitting is anchoring on noise.

2. **Inconclusive ≠ broken.** A null-signal SPRT result (verdict=continue with mean near 0) is a valid landing-time outcome. The roadmap explicitly anticipated this for M5.I. Defer the feature with documented revisit conditions rather than iterating tunables against a null baseline.

3. **The "literature predicts +10-20 Elo" prior doesn't guarantee replication at this engine's strength.** Crafty's two-step widening schedule and Meesha's multi-step schedule are reported as net positive in their respective engine contexts. MadChess removed a three-tier schedule and gained +9 Elo. The M5.I result joins the MadChess data point as null/negative empirical evidence; the literature signal is engine-specific.

4. **Helper-design coupling between caller and helper is a real correctness risk.** Plan-review pass 1 flagged the original `widen_intermediate` signature as having a load-bearing-but-unstated invariant (`prev_alpha = prev − W_SHORT`); pass 2's collapsed signature `(returned, prev_alpha, prev_beta)` eliminated it. Caught by plan review, not by tests.

5. **Bench's depth coverage matters for "bench-unchanged" predictions.** The plan §12 prediction relied on AS11's stable-fixture characterization at depths 4-5; the bench runs at depth 7 where the aspiration loop does engage. Future "bench-unchanged" predictions should reason about the bench's actual depth, not just whether the production code is byte-identical on a different stable position.

## Decision

**M5.I is DEFERRED**, not abandoned. The intermediate aspiration tier may help when:

- Clawfish reaches **depth ≥ 14 at typical TCs** (currently ~depth 8-12). At deeper search, score-continuity strengthens and the tier-2 mechanism's literature payoff is more plausibly realizable. (This mirrors M5.H2's deferral structure: depth-14+ revisit condition.)
- **OR** the engine's move-ordering quality improves substantially (e.g., post-M12 NNUE) so that tier-1 failure rates drop and tier-2 firings become rarer-but-more-targeted.

If a future iteration revisits M5.I, the tuning-backlog entry §"M5.I aspiration third tier" lists the tunable space (width retune, depth-gating, asymmetric widths, score-volatility-adaptive width). The plan + research note can be reactivated without restarting from scratch.
