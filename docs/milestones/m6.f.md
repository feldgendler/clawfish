# M6.F — Tier-1 HCE features (retrospective)

Sixth M6 sub-phase. Lands **three Tier-1 hand-crafted-eval constructs** —
knight/bishop **outposts**, **rook on open/semi-open file** (additive
`(mg,eg)` blend-numerator terms), and **endgame draw-scaling** (the first
*multiplicative* eval construct in the project) — *before* the M6.H joint
Texel pass so they co-calibrate in the one tune (the "extend then tune" law —
Texel cannot discover absent features). **Shipped score-neutral inert from the
start** per the established M6.C/M6.D/M6.E precedent: all additive weights
zeroed + every endgame-scale tunable == `EG_SCALE_DEN` (scale ≡ identity) ⇒
`evaluate` byte-identical to `M6.E` (bench `1213649`/depth-4 `90591`
byte-for-byte). The whole weight/coefficient set defers to the M6.H joint
Texel reshape.

## What landed

- **`src/eval/tier1.rs`** (new) — white-perspective, computed live in
  `evaluate_core`, never cached (the ADR-0033 §6 live-term class; not
  pawn-only):
  - **`outpost_term_white(pos) -> (i32,i32)`** iterating the **`pub(crate)
    outpost_squares(pos, side) -> Bitboard`** seam (the M6.E `king_zone`-seam
    mandate — the structural test pins the *real* code path, not an inline
    reconstruction). `outpost_squares` = `!*_attack_front_spans(enemy_pawns)`
    (the "hole") ∩ the side's immediate pawn-attack set ("supported"),
    per-side. The per-piece loop applies the **`(3..=5)` relative-rank gate**
    (chess ranks 4–6) then indexes the per-kind per-relative-rank
    `OUTPOST_{KNIGHT,BISHOP}_{MG,EG}[rr]` tables. The gate is
    **correctness-load-bearing, not a removable filter**: the
    front-span-complement is a valid "no enemy pawn can ever challenge" test
    *only* for enemy-half squares (a challenging enemy pawn for an enemy-half
    square is necessarily ahead of it; the gate guarantees this).
  - **`rook_file_term_white(pos) -> (i32,i32)`** — `file_fill(own_pawns)` /
    `file_fill(enemy_pawns)` projection; the rook's own square is in the
    projection iff a pawn is on its file (`(own_files &
    Bitboard::from_square(sq)).any()`) — **no per-file mask helper exists or
    is needed**. Open (`!on_own ∧ !on_enemy`) > semi-open (`!on_own`)
    precedence.
  - **`endgame_scale(pos) -> i32`** — returns a numerator over the
    **structural** denominator `EG_SCALE_DEN` (not a tunable);
    `evaluate_core` applies `blended * scale / EG_SCALE_DEN` **before**
    `+ mop_up` (mop-up is the unscaled won-endgame conversion nudge —
    ADR-0034 §5). `scale = EG_SCALE_DEN`, `.min(OCB_WITH_PAWNS_SCALE)` if
    `is_ocb_with_pawns`, `.min(PAWNLESS_DRAW_SCALE)` if `is_pawnless_drawish`,
    the linear 50-move ramp `FIFTY_MOVE_TAPER_FROM..=100`, `.clamp(0,
    EG_SCALE_DEN)`.
  - **`pub(crate) is_ocb_with_pawns`** — exactly one bishop each, on
    **opposite** colour complexes, no queens, no rooks, ≥1 pawn (the M6.A
    `bishop_pair_term_white` `LIGHT_SQUARES`/`DARK_SQUARES` idiom). Disjoint
    by construction from `is_kbkb_same_color`/`is_insufficient_material`.
  - **`pub(crate) is_pawnless_drawish`** — zero pawns/queens/rooks both
    sides, then the **narrow enumerated accept-list**: KNNvK or
    balanced-≤1-minor-each. **KBNvK / KBBvK / any side with `bishops+knights
    ≥ 2` other than the exact KNNvK pattern are explicitly EXCLUDED** (forced
    wins must never be scaled down — the M6.H-active form is a correctness
    feature). The KBvK/KNvK→true overlap with `is_insufficient_material` is
    unobservable in eval (the `eval.rs:228` early-return precedes
    `endgame_scale`); the predicate is tested in isolation.
- **Shipped config: all additive weights zeroed + every scale tunable ==
  `EG_SCALE_DEN`** in `eval::data` (`OUTPOST_*=[0;8]`, `ROOK_*_FILE_*=0`,
  `OCB_WITH_PAWNS_SCALE=PAWNLESS_DRAW_SCALE=FIFTY_MOVE_FLOOR=EG_SCALE_DEN=64`;
  `FIFTY_MOVE_TAPER_FROM=80` + `EG_SCALE_DEN` documented **structural**).
  `const TIER1_IN_EVAL = true`; term math live at the inert config
  (M6.H-ready — the M6.B–E `*_IN_EVAL` precedent). Pre-zero literature
  defaults preserved in the `data.rs` provenance block as the M6.H starting
  point.
- **`evaluate_core` wiring.** The `TIER1_IN_EVAL` block + the blend-numerator
  extension (`+ op_mg + rf_mg` / `+ op_eg + rf_eg`) + `let scaled = blended *
  scale / data::EG_SCALE_DEN;` + `result_white = scaled + mop_up`. The scale
  multiplies the **blend numerator only** — never `static_eval_white()` (the
  RFP/NMP/FFP pruning input) nor the mop-up estimate (ADR-0032 §4). Because
  the scale is the **first multiplicative construct** in M6, "the pruning
  eval is unscaled" is a fresh invariant with its own boundary regression
  test (`endgame_scale_excludes_static_accessor_vs_m6e`) in addition to the
  M6.E-mirrored accessor/mop-up pins.
- No `PawnHashEntry`/`PawnEval`/Zobrist/movegen/make-unmake change — a pure
  additive + scaling `evaluate()` extension reading existing M1 attack fns +
  `Position` occupancy + the M6.B `bitboard::{file_fill,
  *_attack_front_spans}` helpers + the halfmove clock.

## The endgame-scaling inert-vs-live decision (the owned judgment call)

The roadmap recorded endgame-scaling's **inert vs live** disposition as an
explicit open ADR question (not pre-decided), with a recorded secondary
optimizer-tractability argument *reinforcing live*. ADR-0034 §4 resolves it
**inert-per-precedent**, owned explicitly (the M6.E "own the divergence, don't
paper it" discipline). The §4 reasoning honestly concedes
live-with-a-fixed-coefficient **strictly dominates inert on two of three
axes** (optimizer-robustness — it satisfies the M6.H linearity concern
unconditionally; correctness-timing — it delivers the OCB/pawnless fix at
M6.F not M6.H) and that inert wins on **exactly one** (landing-gate
uniformity + reversibility/risk). It selects inert because: (a) the roadmap's
explicit default for this open question is inert-per-precedent; (b) the
reinforcing optimizer argument is *conditional on the still-open M6.H
optimizer ADR* (it bites only gradient/finite-difference, not SPSA) and is
**fully honorable at M6.H without M6.F landing live** — M6.H can fix the
scale coefficients and exclude them from the tunable vector then
(`EG_SCALE_DEN`/`FIFTY_MOVE_TAPER_FROM` are already declared structural for
exactly this); (c) the dominated correctness-timing axis costs **zero
measurable strength** — M6.F→M6.G→M6.H carries no rated play (M6.G is
data-only / no engine build; M6.H's SPRT baseline is the `M6.F` tag), so the
correctness fix is *sequenced* (active at M6.H, the first non-inert build),
not forfeited. The ADR states the auditable revisit trigger if a future
roadmap change inserts rated play into that gap. The decision survived a
3-pass blind plan-review and pass-1 final review.

## Lessons learned

- **For a score-neutral *predicate*, the `pub(crate)` seam is not optional —
  an inline reconstruction pins nothing.** The test-suite review's must-fix
  caught that `outpost_predicate_knight_pawn_supported_hole` rebuilt the
  hole∩support formula inline and never called the term, so a wrong
  span-selector / `& vs |` / sign bug in `outpost_term_white` would be caught
  by *no* test at the inert config (the symbolic tests are M6.H-dormant). The
  fix — extract `pub(crate) outpost_squares(pos, side)`, route the term
  through it, call it from the structural test — is the M6.E `king_zone`-seam
  lesson applied to a *predicate* (not just a zone). The plan-review loop
  converged without catching this; the test-suite review is where it surfaced
  (its core "would this pass against a wrong impl?" job). Mutation testing
  then confirmed zero missed in `tier1.rs`, validating the seam.
- **Don't mischaracterize an M6.H-dormant gate-check as a live
  discriminator.** A pass-2 test-suite-review should-fix caught the
  orchestrator's own plan wording claiming `outpost_rank_gate_structural`'s
  term-level rel-rank-2/6 checks "discriminate a too-wide gate NOW." They do
  not: with out-of-band table entries pinned `0`, a gate-absent impl still
  yields `0·x=(0,0)` (now *and* post-M6.H). The honest framing — the gate's
  correctness rests on the plan §3 design invariant + the bench
  byte-identity gate, the term-level checks only document the fixtures — is
  the M6.E "wrong TDD-red expectation" lesson recurring. The misframing
  originated in an orchestrator plan edit; the blind loop caught it.
- **An owned discretionary decision is stronger when it concedes what it
  loses.** ADR-0034 §4 selects inert *while explicitly stating*
  live-with-fixed-coefficient dominates on 2 of 3 axes and inert wins only on
  uniformity/reversibility. A pass-1 plan-review should-fix asked for exactly
  this honesty (the decision was "framed as a closer call than it is"). The
  decision did not change; its defensibility came from making the trade-off
  auditable rather than rationalized — the M6.E "own, don't paper" discipline
  matured into "own, *and quantify the cost of owning it*."
- **The first multiplicative eval construct needs its own boundary pin.**
  Every prior M6 term was additive; "the additive term doesn't reach the
  pruning eval" was covered by the M6.E-shaped accessor/mop-up tests. A
  multiplicative scale is the first construct that could *scale* the pruning
  input, so `endgame_scale_excludes_static_accessor_vs_m6e` is a genuinely
  new invariant, not a mechanical mirror — and is recorded as such for the
  M6.H ADR's optimizer-linearity reasoning (a fixed-coefficient scale folds
  into the cached feature mask; a tuned one makes the surface bilinear).
