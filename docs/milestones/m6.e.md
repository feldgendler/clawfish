# M6.E — King safety (retrospective)

Fifth M6 sub-phase. Lands the **king-safety evaluation infrastructure**
(king-zone attacker-weight S-curve + castled pawn-shield + MG-only
open/semi-open-file-toward-king penalty) **shipped score-neutral from the
start** — every king-safety weight zeroed in `eval::data`, the entire weight
set deferred to the M6.F joint-Texel reshape. Unlike M6.C/M6.D this phase ran
**no SPRT screen ladder**: the research transfer-risk verdict (HIGH — the
strongest of any M6 term, the PeSTO-king-PST double-count) + the M6.B→C→D
"co-calibrated-elsewhere ≠ transfers here" three-phase law + king-safety's
universally-acknowledged SPRT-noisiness + the *coupled-by-design* components
(no M6.B-style interaction-immune subset to find) make the screen's only
possible outcome "defer," at the highest screen cost of any M6 term. M6.E
lands behaviorally inert vs `M6.D` (bench `1213649` byte-for-byte).

## What landed

- **`king_safety::king_safety_term_white(pos) -> (i32, i32)`** in
  `src/eval/king_safety.rs` — white-perspective; both kings ±sign
  symmetrically; computed live in `evaluate_core` (king square + every
  attacker's square are not pawn-only ⇒ never cached, the ADR-0032 §3 class /
  ADR-0033 §6). Three components per defended king: (a) attacker S-curve —
  per-kind units `Σ KING_ATTACK_WEIGHT[k]·popcount(piece_attacks(sq, occ_all)
  & king_zone)` over enemy N/B/R/Q, gated `attackers < 2 ∨ no enemy queen`,
  indexed `KING_SAFETY_TABLE[units.clamp(0,99)]`, MG-only; (b) castled pawn-
  shield (king file `≥ f ∨ ≤ c`; per king-file±1 file: rank-2 pawn → SHIELD_1,
  else rank-3 → SHIELD_2; relative-rank mirror White r2/r3 ↔ Black r7/r6);
  (c) MG-only open/semi-open king-file + adjacent-file penalty with the
  both-adjacent-semi-open amplifier. Pawn-storm omitted (ADR-0033 §3 —
  documented strategic gap).
- **`pub(crate) fn king_zone(side, ksq) -> Bitboard`** seam — the single
  source of truth for the zone formula `ring | (fwd & !Bitboard::from_square(
  ksq))` (`ring = king_attacks(ksq)`; `fwd = ring.shift_north()` White /
  `shift_south()` Black). The `& !from_square(ksq)` is load-bearing (removes
  the king square the toward-enemy shift re-introduces for a non-back-rank
  king — ADR-0033 §1); 11 squares central / 8 back-rank / 5 corner, king
  square excluded. The term *and* the zone tests both call it (the M6.B
  `pub(crate)` testable-predicate precedent — added in the test-suite review
  loop so the four zone tests pin the real code path, not a reconstruction).
- **Shipped config: all 13 king-safety weights zeroed** in `src/eval/data.rs`

## Why no SPRT screen ladder (the divergence from M6.C/M6.D, owned)

M6.C *ran* its 3-config mixed-TC screen and M6.D *ran* its 6-SPRT per-kind
screen, then each deferred *because the screen empirically returned "defer."*
M6.E skips the diagnostic screen entirely on a **prediction** (research §8) —
a stronger claim than either precedent, owned explicitly (ADR-0033 §8 / plan
§10), not papered as "precedent." Justification: king safety is the
highest-transfer-risk M6 term (PeSTO's MG king PST already prices ~30–50 cp of
castled-king safety — the dominant double-count axis, structurally the M6.D
PST-double-count finding but stronger), it is universally the noisiest eval
term to SPRT (so its screen needs *mixed-TC*, ~5× M6.B's single-TC cost), and
its three components are **coupled by design** (the S-curve danger is fed by
shield weakness and open files — there is no M6.B-style interaction-immune
subset to find, unlike M6.B's CONN-only or M6.D's independent N/B/R/Q axes).
The screen's only possible outcome is "defer," at the highest screen cost of
any M6 term ⇒ negative expected value. Roadmap §M6 line 296 ("each phase
commits its own SPRT-positive change") is satisfied **vacuously** for an inert
landing (eval byte-identical to `M6.D` ⇒ no Elo claim ⇒ SPRT measures zero by
construction — the settled M6.C/M6.D *disposition*).

## Lessons learned

- **The "co-calibrated-elsewhere ≠ transfers here" law matured from
  *empirical* (M6.B→C→D ran the screen) to *predictive* (M6.E skips it).**
  Three screen campaigns established a structural law strong enough to act on
  *before* spending the screen: when (i) the literature term double-counts a
  PeSTO PST axis, (ii) its components are coupled-by-design (no
  interaction-immune subset exists), and (iii) the term is SPRT-noisy enough
  to mandate mixed-TC screens, the screen's only outcome is "defer" and its
  expected value is negative. The cost saved was ~the multi-day mixed-TC
  king-safety screen; the cost paid was owning a prediction rather than an
  empirical result (the ADR/plan own this divergence explicitly, gated by the
  blind plan-review loop + the user reading along, not papered as
  "precedent").
- **A `pub(crate)` seam turns a "tests a copy of the formula"
  non-discrimination into a real pin.** The test-suite review caught that the
  zone tests reconstructed the zone formula inline and never invoked the
  term ⇒ a wrong shift selector / missing king-square mask would pass all
  four. Extracting `king_zone(side, ksq)` as the single source of truth (the
  M6.B `isolated_pawns`/`connected_pawns` precedent) made the structural
  tests exercise the real code path — verified by the coder deliberately
  breaking `king_zone` and confirming 5 tests went red. For a score-neutral
  term where value tests are M6.F-dormant, the weight-free structural seam is
  the *only* current correctness signal beyond the bench gate.
- **For a score-neutral term, "symbolic tests fail against the (0,0) stub" is
  the wrong TDD-red expectation** (the plan §5 wording, inherited from the
  M6.D plan's identical imprecision). A correctly-written symbolic test of a
  *zeroed* term computes `expected = sign·(zeroed eval::data) = (0,0)` and
  legitimately matches the stub. The genuine anti-confirmation-bias property
  is *structural* M6.F-revalidation (independently re-derive from FEN +
  named `eval::data` constants, so the test auto-discriminates the instant
  M6.F sets non-zero weights) + weight-free structural preconditions that
  exercise real code now (the `king_zone` seam, the gate-passing
  precondition). Shipped-build correctness rests on the bench-byte-identity
  landing gate, not these tests — the M6.C/M6.D inert-landing precedent.
- **ADR supersession must land in the structural `## Supersedes` section, not
  only inline.** The plan-review loop caught that ADR-0033's §3-supersession
  of ADR-0032 was recorded inline/Consequences but missing the dedicated
  `## Supersedes` section the ADR-0031 convention establishes — a future
  reader auditing supersession via the structural convention would miss it.
  Immutability bookkeeping has a canonical discoverable place; use it.
